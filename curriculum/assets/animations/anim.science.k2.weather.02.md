## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: the same place changes with the seasons and so do its plants, animals and people; daily weather still varies inside a season (one cold day is not winter); recording the weather day after day is how the pattern shows.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–14s | Meadow: tree in bud, light rain, fox with kits, kid chip shows rain jacket; SPRING badge | "SPRING: warmer, rainy, buds open. The fox has kits. You wear a rain jacket." |
| 14–28s | Same meadow: full green leaves, bright light, sleek fox, sun-hat chip; SUMMER badge | "SUMMER: hot and bright. Green leaves. Thin fox coat. Sun hat." |
| 28–42s | Same meadow: orange leaves falling, fox coat visibly thicker, jacket chip; FALL badge | "FALL: cooling down. Leaves drop. The fox's coat grows thicker. Jacket on." |
| 42–56s | Same meadow: bare tree, snow, fluffy thick fox, coat+scarf chip; WINTER badge | "WINTER: cold. Bare tree, snow. Thickest coat — for the fox AND for you." |
| 56–72s | Back to the green summer tree — but grey clouds and rain over it | "But inside a season, weather still changes day by day. A cold rainy day in SUMMER… is still summer!" |
| 72–90s | A seven-square weather strip fills in: ☀☀🌧☀☁☀🌧 | "How do we know the season for sure? We RECORD the weather — and the pattern shows us." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the SAME meadow/tree/fox in all four beats (continuity is the point); the fixed season order spring→summer→fall→winter; all three effect lanes each season (plant, animal, person); the rainy-summer beat keeping the green tree on screen; the strip as ordinary paper squares.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** never cut to a different place per season (breaks "same place changes"); no fox in human clothes (the coat grows); no faces on the sun, clouds, snowflakes or the weather strip; the sun plain; no scary storm; no confetti.

## What happens next on Goji

Practice: in-package season-detective taps (clue → season, incl. the bare-tree-in-summer and rainy-summer traps) + weather-strip counting; then check `quiz.science.k2.weather.02`; then the K weather journal continues, now season-tagged.
