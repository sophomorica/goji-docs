## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: everything changes over time; some changes are **fast** (watchable — a leaf falls, ice cream melts) and some are **slow** (a seed becomes a tree, a puppy becomes a dog — too slow to watch but never stopped); living things change by growing, nonliving things get changed by waves, wind, or people.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–5s | Tree with an orange leaf that detaches and tumbles down; ice cream cone slumps as a pink puddle spreads | "Some changes are FAST — you can watch them!" |
| 5–13s | Stepped row lights up left to right: acorn → sprout → sapling → full tree (arrows between); small puppy cross-fades to the SAME grown dog | "Some changes are SLOW — too slow to watch, but they never stop." |
| 13–19s | Waves roll over a sandcastle, which settles into a smooth mound; a road with a dashed line and a yellow digger appears (people built it) | "Living things GROW. Nonliving things get CHANGED." — "Waves wore the castle down. People built the road." |
| Close | Three badges lit: Fast changes · Slow changes · Nonliving gets changed | "Everything changes — fast or slow!" |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** slow change as a stepped progression (never an instant morph); the puppy and dog as one individual; a nonliving thing being changed by an outside force (waves) AND one changed by people (road).
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** no faces/eyes on nonliving things (castle, ice cream, crayon, truck, road); no magic "poof" transformations (feeds the all-at-once misconception); never imply the seed or tree is "not changing" while still; no confetti; calm motion.

## What happens next on Goji

Practice tool + module: in-package before/after matching + fast/slow sort (6 pairs, 12 interactions), then the 5-item check; hand-off starts the month-long observation journal (plant a seed or track the moon) with a parent.

## Brand / motion notes

Calm, clear, skippable, replayable; Pi-friendly; Goji warm seal-red/cream chrome. `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses the timeline to stepped stills. No confetti cascades.
