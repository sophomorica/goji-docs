## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: living things grow and develop in **orderly changes** — a **life cycle**. The frog's circle runs egg → tadpole (swims, breathes under water) → froglet (legs grow, tail shrinks, starts breathing air) → frog, which lays NEW eggs; the bean's circle runs seed → sprout → plant → flower → NEW seeds. **Both are circles, both always in the same order, animal and plant alike** — and each starts from a small living beginning (egg or seed) that grows.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–14s | Pond edge, cluster of jelly frog eggs bobbing | "A life cycle goes in ORDER. The frog starts as an EGG." |
| 14–28s | Tadpole swims underwater, bubbles rising | "Egg → TADPOLE. It swims and breathes under water." |
| 28–47s | Froglet with shrinking tail on a lily pad; grown frog beside it hops once; 🔁 mark | "Tadpole → FROGLET → FROG. The frog lays NEW eggs!" |
| 47–64s | Soil cross-section: seed → root/shoot → sprout; then plant with a flower | "Plants too! SEED → SPROUT → PLANT → FLOWER." |
| Close (64–88s) | Two dashed rings side by side — frog stages around one, bean stages around the other — "BOTH in ORDER" between them; open pod shows new seeds | "The flower makes NEW seeds. Both circles, both in order — each starts from a little beginning that grows." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** every stage in sequence with arrows/ring always clockwise (order is the point); the frog laying eggs closing the animal ring and the pod of new seeds closing the plant ring; the two rings on screen TOGETHER at the close (plant and animal alike); egg and seed drawn as same-size small beginnings; tadpole/froglet/frog clearly the same animal changing.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** never run a ring backwards or skip a stage (any-order guard); the plant must visibly CHANGE between stages — never a static plant "just sitting"; the seed must never look like a pebble/rock (seed-not-alive guard — show it waking with root and shoot); no gill/lung anatomy diagrams; frog is a living character (eyes fine) but pots, pods and soil get no faces; no confetti.

## What happens next on Goji

Practice: in-package ring-building — place the frog's 4 stages then the bean's 5 stages into glowing ring slots (out-of-order picks re-teach "same order every time"), then 3 cross-questions (which stage makes the NEW seeds; what follows the tadpole; both start from an egg/seed). Then the 5-item check, then Kiwix "Frog" or the real seed-cup check + journal hand-off.
