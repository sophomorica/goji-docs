## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: an equation is a balance; a variable is the unknown value that makes both sides equal; you isolate the variable by doing the *inverse* operation to both sides; the solution is the value that checks true.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–12s | Balance scale with x-box on left and number tiles; scale is level | "An equation is a balance. Both sides are equal *now* — keep them equal" |
| 12–30s | Equation **x + 4 = 9**: four unit tiles next to x; remove 4 from both sides; x = 5; check by substituting | "Undo addition with subtraction — same move on both sides. Solution: x = 5" |
| 30–48s | Equation **3x = 12**: three identical x-boxes; split into three equal groups; x = 4 | "Undo multiplication with division. The 3 is the *coefficient* — how many x's" |
| 48–62s | Quick flash of x − 5 = 2 and x/2 = 6 with inverse tags | "Whatever was done to x, do the opposite. Addition↔subtraction, multiplication↔division" |
| 62–72s | Vocabulary bar: **variable** · **coefficient** · **solution** lights on the worked equation | "Variable is the unknown. Coefficient multiplies it. Solution makes the equation true" |
| Close | Scale settles level with 5 = 5; end card | "Check it. Then write your own!" |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** both-sides discipline; inverse pairs; check-by-substitution; vocabulary named once on-screen.
- **Avoid:** moving terms with vague "swap sides" without inverse; two-step equations in this brief; implying guessing is the method.
