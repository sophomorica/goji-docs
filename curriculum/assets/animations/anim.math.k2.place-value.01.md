## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: a 2-digit number is a count of ten-bundles plus loose ones — 34 means 3 bundles of ten and 4 loose.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–10s | Goji seal character waves; 34 loose berries scatter on cream background. Counting one-by-one starts and visibly drags | "Counting one at a time takes forever…" |
| 10–30s | Berries snap into rows of ten; each full row wraps into a tidy bundle with a soft click. Three bundles + four loose remain | "Ten friends make a bundle. Bundle… bundle… bundle… and 4 left over" |
| 30–55s | The numeral 34 appears; the 3 glows as the bundles slide under it, the 4 glows as the loose berries slide under it | "The 3 counts the bundles of ten. The 4 counts the loose ones. Thirty-four!" |
| 55–70s | Quick second example: 71 builds itself (7 bundles, 1 loose). Then a teen: 14 = 1 bundle, 4 loose | "14 is a bundle and four — the one is a whole bundle of ten!" |
| Close | Goji seal nods at the two built numbers; gentle end card | "Numbers tell you their bundles. Go build some!" |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the Goji seal as guide; grouping happening (loose → bundle motion); the digit-to-pile link (which digit counts what); one teen example (teens are the misconception hot spot).
- **Avoid:** reading digits as two separate numbers ("three-four"); any carrying/borrowing; more than 2 examples per scene; confetti or HUD-style effects; abstract base-ten blocks without first showing the bundling motion.

## What happens next on Goji

Practice tool: math → mental math (place-value set: "30 + 4", "5 tens = ?"), then `quiz.math.k2.place-value.02`.

## Brand / motion notes

Calm, clear, skippable; Pi-friendly; Goji warm seal-red/cream palette; the Goji seal icon is the recurring guide character across all curriculum animations. No confetti cascades. Respect reduced-motion.
