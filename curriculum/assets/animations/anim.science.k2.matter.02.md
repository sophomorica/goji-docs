## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: everything is **matter** — it has mass and takes up space (even air); **solids keep their shape**, **liquids take the container's shape**, **gases spread out to fill all the space**; **heating** melts solids and boils liquids into gas, **cooling** freezes liquids — and through every change it stays the **same stuff** (melted chocolate is still chocolate).

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–14s | Balance scale: apple on one pan, block on the other | "Everything is MATTER: it has mass and takes up space." |
| 14–26s | Red block beside a glass, then inside the glass — still a block | "SOLIDS keep their shape." |
| 26–41s | One pitcher pours the same juice into a tall glass, a round bowl, a wide dish — three shapes | "LIQUIDS take the container's shape." |
| 41–57s | Translucent balloon inflates; air dots drift apart to fill the whole inside | "GASES spread out to fill ALL the space." |
| Close (57–85s) | Triptych: chocolate bar melting to a shiny puddle over gentle heat · pot boiling with steam wisps rising · ice tray with frost — caption "SAME stuff!" | "Heat melts and boils. Cold freezes. It's the SAME stuff every time." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the SAME juice in all three containers (same color throughout — "same stuff" is visual); air drawn as dots inside the balloon (gas is real, not nothing); melted chocolate visibly still chocolate-brown next to its bar; steam wisps clearly coming from water; the change captions paired with heat 🔥 or cold ❄ as the cause.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** no faces or eyes on blocks, balloons, pots, chocolate (non-living stays non-living); never show sand or powder in the liquids beat (pours-means-liquid guard lives in practice); steam must not look like smoke or vanish into "nothing"; melted chocolate must never change color/identity (no "new stuff"); no confetti; one idea per beat.
