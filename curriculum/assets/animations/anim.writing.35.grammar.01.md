## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: part of speech = the **job** a word is doing in this sentence, not a permanent label — nouns name, verbs power, adjectives and adverbs tune, pronouns substitute, conjunctions couple — and the same word can take different jobs in different sentences.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–12s | Goji seal in a small workshop: a sentence assembles itself as a gentle machine — "The dog chased the ball." Words roll in as parts; the machine hums when the sentence completes | "Every word in a sentence is doing a job. Watch them work" |
| 12–30s | Two stations light up in turn. NAMING station: *dog* and *ball* click into slots shaped like name-tags. ENGINE station: *chased* drops into the engine slot and the sentence moves | "Nouns name the things. The verb is the engine — no verb, no go" |
| 30–48s | TUNING station: *muddy* clips onto *dog* (adjective tunes a noun); *quickly* clips onto *chased* (adverb tunes a verb). Each clip visibly points at the word it tunes. Then SUBSTITUTE bench: *it* swaps in for *the ball*, tag transfers | "Adjectives tune nouns. Adverbs tune verbs. Pronouns? Substitutes — *it* covers for the ball" |
| 48–66s | COUPLER: a second little sentence rolls up ("The cat watched."); *and* couples the two like train cars. Then the reveal: the word *play* walks past two slots — drops into an engine slot ("We **play** outside") and then into a name slot ("The **play** starts") | "Conjunctions couple sentences together. And look — *play* can take EITHER job. The sentence decides!" |
| Close | The seal stamps a work-order card listing the six jobs as tiny icons; machine idles calmly | "Six jobs. Every sentence hires. Go spot who's doing what!" |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the Goji seal as guide; job/role framing throughout (stations, not labels); adjectives-point-at-nouns and adverbs-point-at-verbs shown as physical attachment; one word (*play*) switching jobs — the core insight; the verb as engine (sentence literally doesn't run without it).
- **Avoid:** "verb = action word" as the whole story (the engine metaphor covers *is/was* later without contradiction — no need to teach linking verbs here, just don't define verbs by visible action); all six stations lit at once (introduce sequentially, keep ≤ 2 active); grammar-jargon subtitles (noun/verb/etc. appear as station names only); interjections/prepositions (out of scope for `obj.writing.35.grammar.01`); confetti or HUD effects.
