## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: ecosystems get hurt two ways — by **nature** (fire, flood, disease; with the honest-simple nuance that some fires clear brush and renew a forest) and by **people** (litter in the stream, cutting every tree, wasting water); **erosion** is the soil thief when land is bare (bare hill + rain → muddy stream); **conservation** is the set of counter-moves — plant cover, use less water, keep streams clean, replant — and every counter-move has a kid-sized version.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–10s | Three calm panels: low brush fire (green shoots beyond), flooded meadow, wilting spotted plants | "Nature can hurt: fire, flood, disease. But some fires clear old brush and renew." |
| 10–20s | Three panels: litter drifting in a stream, hillside of stumps, tap running to waste | "People can hurt too: litter, every tree cut, wasted water." |
| 20–32s | Bare hill in rain; topsoil streaks slide down; clear stream turns muddy | "EROSION — the soil thief. Bare land + rain = the best layer stolen." |
| 32–44s | Four counter-move panels: grass rows holding a slope, tap closing, clean stream with fish, seedling planted | "CONSERVATION = counter-moves: plant cover, use less water, clean streams, replant." |
| Close (44–56s) | The child caretaker waters a young tree by a clean river at the jungle's edge; stamp CARETAKERS AT WORK | "Every move is kid-sized. The jungle stays healthy when its caretakers tend it — that includes you." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the fire nuance shown, not just told (green shoots visible beyond the burned strip); erosion as a visible transfer (soil leaves the hill AND the stream darkens — the same event seen at both ends); every counter-move paired to the hurt it counters; at least one counter-move performed by a child's hands.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** nothing frightening — no walls of flame, no drowning or injured animals, no gore (a 3rd grader is being recruited, not scared); never frame fire as simply fine (the nuance is "some fires renew," not "fire is good"); no faces on clouds, water, fire or soil; no guilt-heavy imagery; erosion never shown only in a desert (feeds "erosion only happens in deserts"); no confetti.

## What happens next on Goji

Then the 5-item check, then the family water-saving pick (journal) + Kiwix "Erosion" → notebooks.
