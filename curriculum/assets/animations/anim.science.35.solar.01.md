## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: **rotate = spin** (one spin = one day and night) and **revolve = travel around the sun** (one trip = one year) are two different motions with two different effects; the eight planets circle the sun in one fixed order (each with a hook fact); the inner four are small and rocky, the outer four gas giants; the sun is a star absurdly bigger than any planet (beach ball vs peppercorn); real planets sit far apart — posters squeeze them.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–9s | Earth (no face) spinning on a tilted dashed axis, sunlit half bright, night half shadowed; curved golden spin arrow | "Earth ROTATES — spins like a top. One spin = one day and one night." |
| 9–18s | Plain sun at center, dashed orbit ring, small Earth gliding along it with a direction arrow | "Earth also REVOLVES — travels all the way around the sun. One trip = one whole YEAR. Rotate = spin. Revolve = travel." |
| 18–29s | Eight planets in a row from a sun sliver, each glowing in turn, name labels; sizes relative | "In order: Mercury scorched and swift · Venus hottest, thick clouds · Earth our home · Mars rusty red · Jupiter biggest · Saturn shining rings · Uranus tipped over · Neptune far, blue, windy." |
| 29–37s | Inner four bracketed small ("rocky"), outer four bracketed huge ("gas giants") | "Inner four: small and rocky. Outer four: gas giants — no ground to stand on." |
| 37–46s | Giant beach-ball sun filling half the frame; a single peppercorn dot labeled Earth | "The sun is a STAR — absurdly bigger. Sun = beach ball, Earth = peppercorn. A million Earths would fit inside." |
| Close | A few tiny dots separated by vast black gaps, trailing "···→" | "Posters squeeze the planets together. Real space is far, FAR apart — mostly empty." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the two motions as visibly different (spin-in-place vs travel-along-path) — the word pair rotate/revolve is the heart; the fixed order left-to-right from the sun; the outrageous sun-to-Earth size ratio; the emptiness of real spacing.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** NO faces on the sun, planets, or stars; never show the sun orbiting Earth or the sun "moving" across the sky as the cause of sunrise; never show the sun only slightly bigger than the planets; never present tight poster-style spacing as reality (it appears only to be debunked); no confetti; calm motion only.
