## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: re-cutting a fraction changes the *names* (more, smaller pieces) but never the *amount* — so one amount wears many fraction names, and you can generate them by cutting every piece the same way.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–12s | Goji seal and a friend, two identical cakes. Cake A cut in 2, seal shades 1 piece: **1/2**. Cake B cut in 4, friend shades 2: **2/4** | "I ate one of two pieces. She ate two of four. Who got more cake?" |
| 12–30s | The shaded parts lift off both cakes and slide together — they overlap *exactly*, edge to edge, held for a beat | "Look — exactly the same amount of cake. 1/2 and 2/4 are the same amount wearing different names" |
| 30–48s | Back to cake A: a knife re-cuts it once (2→4 pieces, shading now 2/4), then again (4→8, shading 4/8). The shaded region never changes, only the cut lines multiply | "Cut every piece again: the pieces double, the shaded ones double too. The amount never moves. 1/2… 2/4… 4/8" |
| 48–65s | Trap scene: **2/3** and **2/4** side by side, both with 2 shaded. Overlap test runs again — this time the pieces *don't* match; 2/3 visibly larger | "Careful! Same top number is not same amount. Two *big* pieces beat two *smaller* pieces. The overlap test never lies" |
| Close | The family 1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8 lined up, amounts pulsing in sync; Goji seal nods | "Same amount, different cuts. Go find some twins!" |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the overlap/superposition proof (amounts physically coinciding) — the visual argument, not the rule; the re-cut where cut lines multiply while shading holds still; one non-example (2/3 vs 2/4) tested by the same overlap; notation appearing *after* each visual, never before.
- **Avoid:** stating "multiply top and bottom by the same number" as the headline rule (the rule may appear as a whispered footnote at the close, never as the lesson); circle-only models (use the rectangular cake/strip — circles hide equal cuts); implying more pieces = more amount anywhere; confetti or HUD effects.
