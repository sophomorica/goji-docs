## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: we use water every day (drink, wash, grow); we find it as rain, in rivers, lakes and the ocean; water has three forms — ice, liquid, steam — and **all three are the same water**; and water always flows **downhill**.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–13s | Glass of water, watering can, bean seedling pop in turn | "We USE water: we drink it, we wash, plants drink it to GROW." |
| 13–28s | Panorama: rain cloud → river → lake → ocean | "We FIND it: rain falls, rivers run into lakes and the big OCEAN." |
| 28–45s | Ice cube, puddle, steaming pot in a row, each popping in turn | "COLD freezes it → ICE. WARM melts it. VERY HOT → STEAM!" |
| 45–55s | Same three, stamp ALL THREE ARE WATER | "Ice is water. Steam is water. Same water — three forms!" |
| Close (55–80s) | Single drop rolls down a green slope to the low ground; stamp NEVER UPHILL | "Water always flows DOWNHILL. It finds the low path." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** ice, liquid and steam explicitly connected as the same water (arrows / stamp); the drop's whole path strictly descending; rain arriving before river/lake/ocean (where water comes from).
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** no faces on clouds, drops, sun, or any object (nothing non-living may look alive); steam never drawn as smoke and never with fire visible (steam ≠ smoke, and no hot-stove modeling for a K child); never show water moving upward anywhere, even decoratively (no fountains); no confetti.

## What happens next on Goji

Practice: in-package "which form is this?" tap rounds (ice cube / puddle / steam / icicles / rain) + three "which way will it flow?" predict-then-test slopes. Then the 5-item check, then the kitchen ice-melt watch + journal hand-off.

## Brand / motion notes

Calm, clear, skippable; Pi-friendly; Goji warm seal-red/cream chrome. `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses beats to stepped stills. No confetti cascades.
