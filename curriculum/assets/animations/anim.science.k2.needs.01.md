## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: every animal needs five things to live — food, water, shelter, air, and space — and things people enjoy (toys, TV, candy) are not needs.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–11s | Lion on the savanna eats meat; FOOD badge lights | "Animals need FOOD. The lion eats meat." |
| 11–22s | Lion drinks at the waterhole; WATER badge | "Animals need WATER. She drinks at the waterhole." |
| 22–33s | Lion rests in the shade of rocks and tall grass; SHELTER badge | "Animals need SHELTER — a safe place. For a lion: shade and rocks. Not a house!" |
| 33–44s | Lion's chest rises and falls, soft breeze lines; AIR badge | "Animals need AIR. In… and out. You can't see it, but it's a need." |
| 44–55s | Wide savanna, lion roams; SPACE badge | "Animals need SPACE — room to roam." |
| 55–70s | A toy ball rolls in; tested against all five badges, each shakes "no" | "A toy? It's not food, not water, not shelter, not air, not space. The lion does NOT need it." |
| Close | Five badges glow around the lion | "Food, water, shelter, air, space — five needs, every animal." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** all five badges earned one at a time; shelter shown as shade/rocks/grass; the air beat made visible (breathing, breeze); the toy failing every badge.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** never draw shelter as a human house with walls; no toy/TV/candy shown as something the lion enjoys; no faces/eyes on the toy, TV, candy, rocks, or waterhole; the lion calm and non-scary (storybook, no bared teeth at the child); no confetti.
