## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: halves are 2 **equal** pieces, fourths are 4 **equal** pieces — and a share is only fair when the pieces are equal.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–10s | Goji seal and a friend split a round cookie. First cut is wildly uneven; the friend's face falls | "Is that fair? No way!" |
| 10–25s | The cut redraws down the middle; both pieces pulse to show they match. Word: HALF | "Two pieces, exactly the same — halves! Fair." |
| 25–40s | Two more friends arrive. The two halves each split again into four matching pieces. Word: FOURTH | "Four friends, four equal pieces — fourths!" |
| 40–52s | Trap scene: a cookie cut into 4 unequal blobs vs 2 perfect halves. Goji seal picks… the bigger *half* | "More pieces doesn't mean more cookie. Equal is what matters." |
| Close | Halves and fourths side by side, labeled | "Fair shares: equal pieces. Go practice!" |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the unfair cut FIRST (emotional hook); equality shown by pieces visibly matching; the more-pieces-isn't-more trap; both a shape (cookie) and, briefly, a set (4 berries shared by 2).
- **Avoid:** fraction notation (½, ¼) as the focus — words first at this band; any pie-chart abstraction before the food metaphor lands; implying fourths are "better" than halves.

## What happens next on Goji
