## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: soil is a **mix** of four ingredients (rock bits, humus from rotted leaves, water, air); nature makes it **very slowly** (rock crumbles, leaves rot — hundreds of years); it sits in **layers** with dark topsoil richest; it **holds and feeds** plants (roots grip + drink nutrients); and the animals living in it (worms, moles, roly-polys) make it **better** — worm tunnels bring air and mix the soil.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–10s | Magnifying circle over dark soil; four ingredient groups glint in turn: rock bits, humus crumbs, water drop, air pockets | "Soil is a MIX: rock bits + humus + water + air." |
| 10–20s | Boulder sheds grit; leaves darken and sink into crumbs; long winding path = long time | "Made SLOWLY: rock crumbles, leaves rot — over many, many years." |
| 20–30s | Cutaway: topsoil (grass, roots) / subsoil / solid rock | "Layers: topsoil on top — dark and richest." |
| 30–42s | Plant in wind: roots grip; golden nutrient specks drift to roots | "Soil HOLDS plants and FEEDS them nutrients." |
| Close (42–55s) | Worm inches through tunnel, mole digs, roly-poly nibbles; air pockets appear along tunnels; stamp A MIX, MADE SLOWLY | "The diggers are helpers: tunnels let air in and mix the soil." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** all four ingredients visible in one magnified view (mix, not one substance); the slowness cue (crumbling stages, not an instant transformation); roots feeding only in the topsoil band; worm tunnels explicitly connected to air pockets and mixing.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** never show soil forming quickly (no time-lapse that reads as days); no faces or eyes on soil, rocks, drops or bubbles; worms and moles never drawn as villains or pests (feeds "worms wreck soil"); humus never drawn as trash bags or litter; no confetti.
