## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: the water cycle follows the SAME drop forever — the sun heats the sea and the drop rises as invisible gas (**evaporation**, liquid→gas); cold high air turns gas back into tiny droplets that form a cloud (**condensation**, gas→liquid — a cloud is water, not smoke/cotton); heavy droplets fall as rain or snow (**precipitation**); the water flows downhill through rivers back to the sea — a circle whose engine is the **sun**; most of Earth's water waits in the ocean, the rest in lakes, rivers, underground and ice; rain is never new water.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–9s | Sun over the sea; the followed drop (glow, NO face) lifts off as vapor streaks | "The sun heats the sea. The drop rises as invisible gas — EVAPORATION." |
| 9–18s | High cold sky; droplets cluster into a plain cloud, the followed drop among them | "Cold turns gas back to droplets — CONDENSATION. A cloud is water!" |
| 18–27s | Droplets bunch, grow heavy; rain streaks fall, snowflakes at the cold edge | "Heavy droplets FALL — PRECIPITATION: rain or snow." |
| 27–38s | The full loop in one frame: sea → up → cloud → mountain rain → river downhill → sea; arrows light in turn | "Downhill, river, sea — a circle with no end. The SUN is its engine." |
| Close (38–50s) | Panorama: huge ocean front and center; smaller lake, river, underground cutaway, glacier; stamp SAME WATER, ROUND AND ROUND | "Most water waits in the ocean. And it is always the same water — a dinosaur may have drunk your drop!" |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** one continuous drop followed through all beats (marked by glow); the three processes named WITH their direction (up as gas / gas→droplets / falling); the sun explicitly credited as the engine; the return leg strictly downhill; ocean visibly biggest among the reservoirs.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** NO faces or eyes on clouds, drops, sun, or water — clouds stay plain (nothing nonliving may look alive); clouds never drawn as smoke or cotton; no water rising already-visible as droplets during evaporation (the gas is invisible — use fading streaks); never show water vanishing to nothing (feeds "water is destroyed"); no "new water" imagery in the cloud; no confetti.
