## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: letters carry sounds, and sliding the sounds together left-to-right *is* reading the word.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–10s | Goji seal finds a "locked" word: F-O-X (from the fables). Each letter is a little tile with a keyhole | "Words are a secret code. Want the key?" |
| 10–30s | Each tile plays its sound as it lights: /f/ … /o/ … /x/. Then the tiles slide together, sounds speeding up until they fuse: "fox!" A fox from Aesop pops out | "Say each sound… then slide them together. Fff-o-x… fox!" |
| 30–50s | Two more anchor words unlock the same way: S-U-N, H-E-N. The slide gesture becomes visibly the same move each time | "Same key, every word. Slide the sounds!" |
| Close | A whole row of word-tiles from the fable, one glows: "You can open every one" | "Go crack some codes!" |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** left-to-right motion every time; sounds *blending* (not three separate sounds then a magic answer); anchor-book words (fox, hen) so the animation's words appear in tonight's fable.
- **Avoid:** letter *names* (cee-ay-tee) — sounds only; whole-word guessing from the picture (picture appears AFTER the blend); more than 3 example words.

## What happens next on Goji

Practice tool: flashcards deck `read-k2-cvc` (anchor-sampled), read aloud to parent; then typing the same words.

## Brand / motion notes

Calm, clear, skippable; Pi-friendly; seal-red/cream; Goji seal as recurring guide. No confetti. Respect reduced-motion.
