## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: the moon revolves around Earth while Earth revolves around the sun; the moon makes no light (it mirrors sunlight); the four major phases (new → first quarter → full → last quarter) are our changing view of the moon's lit half — the lamp-and-ball picture; **seasons come from Earth's tilt, not distance** (Earth is even slightly farther in northern summer); the moon's pull raises the tides twice a day; the moon is closer, smaller, airless, and cratered.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–9s | Sun at left; Earth on a faint orbit arc; small moon circling Earth on a dashed ring (animated lap) | "Two dances at once: the moon circles EARTH — about a month a lap — while Earth circles the sun." |
| 9–17s | Sunbeams from the left strike a half-lit moon; far half dark | "The moon makes NO light of its own. Moonlight is bounced sunlight — a mirror in the sky." |
| 17–28s | Classic lamp-and-ball diagram: Earth center, sun arrows from left, four moon positions lighting in turn with labels new / first quarter / full / last quarter | "Same moon — we see its lit half from different angles. New… first quarter… full… last quarter… and around again." |
| 28–39s | Sun center; two tilted Earths on opposite orbit sides, axes parallel; the toward-leaning half glows warm | "Seasons come from the TILT — not distance! Your half leans toward the sun: summer. Leans away: winter. (Earth is even slightly FARTHER in our summer.)" |
| 39–47s | Night shore; sea gently swells toward the moon and settles (twice) | "The moon's pull tugs the sea — high tide, low tide, about twice a day." |
| Close | Earth beside a quarter-width cratered moon; fact list: closer · no air · old rock | "Closer than anything, airless, cratered — a footprint there lasts millions of years." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** BOTH orbits at once (moon-around-Earth nested in Earth-around-sun); the lamp-and-ball angle explanation for phases; the two tilted Earths at **equal distance** from the sun (equidistance is the argument); the sea responding to the moon's position.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** NO faces on moon, sun, or Earth; never show Earth's shadow creeping across the moon as the cause of phases (no eclipse imagery at all); never draw summer-Earth closer to the sun; the moon must never be drawn glowing without incoming sunbeams; no confetti; calm motion only.
- **Animation candidate note:** this unit is the band's top candidate for a richer generated animation (real lamp-and-ball camera move) when clip budget lands — the four `assets.json` clips cover the interim.
