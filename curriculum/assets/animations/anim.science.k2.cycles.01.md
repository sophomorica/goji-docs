## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: a life cycle is a circle of stages that keeps going (the adult lays eggs and it starts again); the same pattern repeats across animals; and babies look like their parents but are not identical copies.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–12s | A tiny egg on a leaf; first node of a circle lights | "It starts with a tiny egg on a leaf." |
| 12–24s | A caterpillar munches the leaf; second node | "Out hatches… a caterpillar! It eats and eats and grows." |
| 24–36s | The caterpillar hangs and becomes a chrysalis; third node | "Then it makes a chrysalis and rests inside." |
| 36–48s | A butterfly emerges, wings open slowly; fourth node | "Out comes… a butterfly! Same animal — all grown up." |
| 48–60s | The butterfly lays eggs; the loop arrow closes the circle and the ring glows around | "She lays eggs — and the circle goes round again. It never stops!" |
| 60–74s | Quick frog circle beside it: egg → tadpole → frog, loop arrow | "The frog goes round too: egg… tadpole… frog. Same circle, different animal." |
| 74–85s | A mother dog with three puppies — same coloring, different spots | "Puppies look like their mother… but look close — different spots! Like her, not copies." |
| Close | Both circles spin slowly side by side | "Round and round — every animal's life is a circle." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the circle drawn as a CIRCLE with a loop arrow that visibly closes it; the caterpillar→butterfly identity ("same animal"); the frog echo to prove the pattern; the puppies' same-kind-different-spots close-up.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** never run a cycle backwards; never end on the adult without the loop arrow; no mini-butterfly hatching from the egg; puppies never identical clones; no faces/eyes on eggs or the chrysalis; no confetti.
