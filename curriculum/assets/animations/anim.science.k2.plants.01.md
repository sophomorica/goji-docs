## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: every plant part has a **job** — **roots** drink water (with nutrients) and hold the plant tight, the **stem** carries the water up and holds the plant up, **leaves** make the plant's food from **light and air** (plants do NOT eat soil), the **flower** makes **seeds** for new plants (a job, not a decoration) — and a plant that has all its needs (water, light, air, soil, a place to grow) stands tall.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–16s | Cutaway view: soil below, one bean plant; the ROOTS glow; blue water drops travel from the soil INTO the roots and upward; roots grip as a gust leans the plant and it holds | "Meet the ROOTS. Two jobs! They DRINK water from the soil — and they hold the plant tight, even in wind." |
| 16–30s | The STEM glows; the water drops continue climbing up inside the stem to the top; the stem stands straight, holding the leaves high | "The STEM is the water road. It carries water UP — and it holds the plant up tall." |
| 30–48s | The LEAVES glow; sun rays touch them, small air swirls drift in; a tiny food-spark travels from the leaf into the plant; a crossed-out soil-on-a-plate icon | "The LEAVES are the kitchen! They make the plant's FOOD — from light and air. Plants do not eat soil. The leaves MAKE the food." |
| 48–62s | The FLOWER glows; petals open; seeds form and drop to the soil; one seed sprouts into a tiny new plant; stamp SEEDS = NEW PLANTS | "The FLOWER has a job too. It makes SEEDS — and seeds grow into NEW plants!" |
| Close (62–88s) | A second plant droops, leaves down; one by one its needs arrive — water can, sun, air swirl, fresh soil, a bigger pot (place to grow) — and it lifts back upright; five need-icons line up as badges | "This plant is droopy. It needs: water… light… air… soil… and a place to grow. There! Every need met — and it stands tall again." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** water drawn traveling soil → roots → stem → leaves as one continuous path (the drinking route is the skeleton of the whole lesson); the leaves' food made visibly FROM sun rays + air swirls; the flower's seeds falling and sprouting (job, not decoration); the wilting plant recovering only AFTER its needs arrive; all five needs shown, air included.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** no mouth, teeth, faces or eyes on the plant, sun, or any object (and never roots "munching" soil — they drink); no candy or toys appearing as plant needs in the teach (they appear only in practice as wrong answers); no instant recovery before the needs arrive; one part glowing at a time; no confetti; plain cream sky, simple soil cutaway.

## What happens next on Goji

Practice: in-package tap-the-part-that-does-this-JOB rounds (drinks? carries? makes food? makes seeds? holds tight?) then give-the-plant-what-it-needs rounds (sun lamp, water, soil, air vs candy, toy — echoing `obj.science.k2.needs.01`). Then the 5-item check, then planting a real seed cup with the parent → journal sentence and ongoing growth log.

## Brand / motion notes

Calm, clear, skippable; Pi-friendly; Goji warm seal-red/cream chrome. `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses beats to stepped stills (water path drawn as a static dotted route). No confetti cascades.
