## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: what we use comes from the Earth (water, wood, food); when we're done we choose trash / **reuse** (use again) / **recycle** (made into something new); trash rides the truck to a landfill and does not disappear; our choices touch water, land, air, and animals — and one person's choice matters.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–4.5s | The Earth gives: a flowing stream, a tree with cut planks stacked beside it, a garden row of carrots | "Everything we use comes from the EARTH." |
| 4.5–13s | Three bins (grey TRASH, gold REUSE, green RECYCLE with arrow loop); a glass jar drifts down into REUSE; a green garbage truck rolls off toward a landfill mound | "When we're done, we CHOOSE: trash, REUSE, or RECYCLE." · "The jar can be used AGAIN — reuse!" · "Trash rides the truck to a LANDFILL — it does not disappear." |
| 13–17s | Split scene: clean stream with swimming fish + heron vs littered stream (can, bottle, wrapper), murky and EMPTY — "Where did everyone go?" | "Our choices touch the water, land, air — and the animals." |
| Close | Three badges lit: Earth gives · Reuse & recycle · Choices matter | "Every choice matters — even one wrapper." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the origin chain (Earth → the things we use); reuse and recycle as DIFFERENT actions (jar used again vs arrow-loop "made into new"); the landfill as a real place trash goes; the littered stream told by absence (animals gone), gently.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** no faces on bins, truck, trash, jar, or the Earth; trash must never vanish, poof, or "go away" — it visibly rides to the landfill; no dead/suffering animals, no scary pollution imagery (empty + murky is enough); no guilt framing; no confetti; calm motion.

## What happens next on Goji

Practice tool + module: in-package 3-bin sort (drag or tap: trash/reuse/recycle) + two help-the-stream scenario taps, then the 5-item check; hand-off is the home reuse hunt (find 3 things to reuse with a parent) → journal, with the Kiwix "Recycling" article as the parent read.
