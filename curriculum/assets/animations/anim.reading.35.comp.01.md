## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: the main idea is one umbrella sentence that keeps all the important details dry — too big covers nothing, too small leaves details in the rain.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–10s | Rain of detail-drops from a Jungle Book story: *wolves adopt a baby*, *Shere Khan wants him*, *the pack votes*. Goji seal opens an umbrella labeled "ANIMALS" — comically huge, floats away | "'It's about animals'? That covers everything… so it holds nothing" |
| 10–30s | Next umbrella: "MOWGLI SAID A WORD" — tiny; the big details get soaked | "Too small! Look at all the important stuff getting wet" |
| 30–55s | Right-sized umbrella clicks open: "A wolf pack decides to raise a human boy as its own." Each detail-drop lands under it with a satisfying tap | "Just right: every important detail fits underneath" |
| Close | Three umbrellas side by side — too big / too small / just right | "Test your umbrella: does every detail fit? Does nothing extra?" |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** both failure modes (too broad AND too narrow) before the fix; the fit-test (details literally standing under the sentence); a real anchor-book example.
- **Avoid:** "main idea = first sentence" (common false shortcut); topic and main idea used interchangeably — topic is the umbrella *handle* at most, one word; the idea is the sentence.

## What happens next on Goji

Practice tool: reader (Jungle Book story chunk) → journal umbrella sentence + 3 details → story quiz with too-big/too-small trap answers.

## Brand / motion notes

Calm, clear, skippable; Pi-friendly; seal-red/cream; Goji seal guide. No confetti. Respect reduced-motion.
