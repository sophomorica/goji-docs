## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: each sense has its own body part — eyes see, ears hear, nose smells, tongue tastes, hands feel — and a scene can be solved by asking "which body part could notice this?"

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–10s | Rainbow arcs fade in over a meadow; eye emblem lights; badge "Eyes see" turns on | "Your EYES see. A rainbow! Only eyes can catch colors." |
| 10–22s | Bird on a branch, music notes drift up; ear emblem lights; badge on | "Your EARS hear. A bird sings — even when you can't see it." |
| 22–34s | Bread loaf, steam wisps rise; nose emblem; badge on | "Your NOSE smells. Mmm — warm bread!" |
| 34–46s | Red apple, one bite gone; tongue emblem; badge on | "Your TONGUE tastes. Sweet apple!" |
| 46–58s | Fluffy cat, tail sways; hand emblem; badge on | "Your HANDS feel. So soft and fluffy!" |
| Close | All five badges glow in a row | "Five senses — your body's detective team!" |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** one clear body-part emblem per sense; the sound beat plays with the bird partly hidden (you don't need eyes for it); all five badges together at the close.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** no faces/eyes on non-living things (bread, rainbow, notes stay plain); never pair a sense with the wrong organ even as a joke; don't show tasting-by-smelling (kept simple at K); no anatomy diagrams; no confetti.

## What happens next on Goji

Practice: in-package sense sort (drag scenario cards to body-part bins, popcorn multi-sense item); then check `quiz.science.k2.senses.01`; then off-device parent sense walk → journal sentence.

## Brand / motion notes

Calm, clear, skippable, replayable; Pi-friendly; Goji seal-red/cream chrome; `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses beats to stepped stills. No confetti cascades.
