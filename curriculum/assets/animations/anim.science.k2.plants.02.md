## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: everyday products come from plants (wood, cotton, wheat-bread, fruit, paper); plants give animals fresh air, homes, and food; and plant roots hold the soil against rain — so we take care of plants.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–19s | Kitchen still life: wooden table, cotton shirt, bread, apple, paper, each spotlighted; THINGS-WE-USE badge | "The table was a tree. The shirt — a cotton plant. Bread — wheat. Paper — a tree again. Plant things are everywhere." |
| 19–33s | Leafy oak with soft air ribbons drifting from its crown; FRESH-AIR badge | "Plants freshen the air we breathe. Take a deep breath — thank a plant!" |
| 33–47s | Same oak: bird nest on a branch, squirrel with an acorn; HOMES & FOOD badge | "The bird nests in the oak. The squirrel eats its acorns. Home and dinner, both at once." |
| 47–67s | Two hills in the rain: planted hill holds, bare hill's soil streams away; ROOTS badge | "Roots grip the soil like tiny hands. The planted hill stays. The bare hill washes away, down and gone." |
| 67–85s | Hands planting a seedling beside a row of young trees | "That's why we plant new trees when we cut. Cut every tree and plant nothing — we'd lose every gift at once." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** each product traced back to its plant by name (wheat→bread, cacao→chocolate in practice); the air gift kept simple ("freshen the air we breathe" — no gas-exchange mechanism); the two-hills contrast under the *same* rain; the care beat ending on replanting (hope, not doom).
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** never group wool with plant products (soft ≠ plant — wool is the sheep's); never show glass or metal coming from plants (natural ≠ plant); never dismiss bread/paper as "not plants"; no cutting-is-harmless framing — but also no scary clear-cut destruction (calm stumps + runoff, answered by replanting); no faces/eyes on objects, hills, clouds, or tools; no confetti.
