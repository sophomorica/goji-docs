## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: an ecosystem is **all the living things in a place PLUS the nonliving things they use** (sun, water, soil, air); different places (jungle / river / dry grassland) are different ecosystems with different residents; and organisms relate three ways — **eat** (python→deer, deer→grass), **help** (bee↔flower), **compete** (tiger vs python for the same deer).

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–8s | Jungle panorama, living residents highlighted: tiger, deer, monkey, bee, trees, flower | "An ECOSYSTEM is all the living things in one place." |
| 8–16s | Same scene; sun, river, soil, air glow warm; deer drinks, tree roots reach into soil | "PLUS the nonliving things they use: sun, water, soil, air." |
| 16–28s | Three panels: jungle / river (fish, frog) / dry grassland (antelope, hot sun) | "Different places, different ecosystems, different residents." |
| 28–44s | Three pairs with arrows: python→deer (EATS), bee↔flower (HELP, two-way arrows), tiger + python both facing one deer (COMPETE) | "Eat. Help. Compete. The residents are connected." |
| Close (44–60s) | Full scene reassembles; stamp LIVING + NONLIVING | "Living + nonliving + their connections = one team. Mowgli's jungle is one big team." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** nonliving parts (sun, water, soil, air) explicitly counted INTO the ecosystem (glow + stamp); at least one plant as the thing being eaten (deer→grass — eating isn't only predators); the compete pair wanting the SAME prey, not fighting each other.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** never draw the ecosystem as animals only; no faces or eyes on sun, water, soil, air or clouds (nothing nonliving may look alive); no gore in the eat/compete beats (tension, not blood); the tiger must not eat the python (feeds "every big animal eats every small one"); no confetti.

## What happens next on Goji

Practice: in-package "Build the jungle" belongs/doesn't-belong taps (rejecting the polar bear and cactus — living, wrong ecosystem) + six "eat, help, or compete?" pair taps. Then the 5-item check, then Kiwix "Jungle"/"Rainforest" → notebooks (two facts: one living, one nonliving part).
