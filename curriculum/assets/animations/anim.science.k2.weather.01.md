## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: day and night repeat in a cycle (the sun always comes back); the four seasons repeat **in a fixed order** around a wheel; daily weather changes but can be recorded to reveal patterns — and one day's weather is not the season.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–8s | Plain sun (no face) arcs up and across the sky, sets; sky darkens; crescent moon + twinkling stars; then dawn returns and the sun climbs again | "The sun comes up… Day! …then night… …then DAY again. It repeats!" |
| 8–16s | Season wheel (spring green / summer gold / fall orange / winter blue quarters, emoji markers); red arrow sweeps clockwise, lighting each quarter in order, loops past winter back to spring | "Seasons go in ORDER, around the wheel. …then SPRING again!" |
| 16–21s | A week strip (Mon–Sun boxes); sun/sun/cloud/rain/rain/cloud/sun icons stamp in one per day | "Weather changes each day — so we WRITE IT DOWN." |
| Close | Three badges lit: Day–night repeats · Seasons in order · Record the weather | "Look — a pattern! One rainy day does NOT change the season." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the loop closing (night returns to day; winter returns to spring) — the *repeat* is the concept; seasons in one fixed clockwise order every time; the journal strip as the way patterns become visible.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** NO faces on sun, moon, stars, clouds, or rain (no smiley sun); never show seasons out of order or shuffled; night must not read as the sun being destroyed/turned off (it sets and returns); don't equate one weather day with a season; no confetti; calm motion only.

## What happens next on Goji

Practice tool + module: in-package pattern wheel (place the seasons), what-comes-next strips, season-match cards, then the 5-item check; hand-off starts the daily weather-journal habit in the journal app with a parent.

## Brand / motion notes

Calm, clear, skippable, replayable; Pi-friendly; Goji warm seal-red/cream chrome. `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses the timeline to stepped stills. No confetti cascades.
