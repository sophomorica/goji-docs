## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: you don't have to count everything — start big and count on, know your doubles, know the pairs that make ten, and every addition fact hides a subtraction fact.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–15s | Goji seal at 2 + 7. Slow path: counting all nine berries one by one, visibly tedious. Fast path: pocket the 7, count on "8, 9" | "Start with the BIG number. Then just count on: eight… nine!" |
| 15–35s | Doubles parade: 3+3, 4+4, 5+5 as mirror-image berry stacks — instantly recognizable symmetry | "Doubles are twins. You don't count twins — you *know* them" |
| 35–55s | Ten-frame fills: 6+4, 7+3, 8+2 snap satisfyingly into a full frame | "Some pairs are best friends — they make ten every time" |
| 55–80s | Fact family house: 4, 5, 9 live together. 4+5=9 flips to 9−4=5 and 9−5=4, same three berry piles rearranging | "If you know 4+5=9, you already know 9−4. Subtraction is addition wearing a mask!" |
| Close | Goji seal taps a growing "facts I just know" shelf | "Every practice makes the shelf bigger. Go play!" |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** slow-vs-fast contrast for counting-on; doubles symmetry; full ten-frame snap; the addition↔subtraction flip using the *same* objects.
- **Avoid:** timers, stopwatch pressure, or speed-shaming (accuracy first is the product stance); more than 10 objects on screen; introducing facts beyond 10.

## What happens next on Goji

Practice tool: math → addition drill (then subtraction drill), 20 problems, ≥ 90% gate. Remediation: doubles-only flashcard deck.

## Brand / motion notes

Calm, clear, skippable; Pi-friendly; seal-red/cream; Goji seal as recurring guide. No confetti. Respect reduced-motion.
