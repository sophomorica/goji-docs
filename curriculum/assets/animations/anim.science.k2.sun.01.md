## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: the sun warms the land, the air, and the water; a shadow happens when something **blocks** the sun's light; the shadow falls on the side **away** from the sun and moves when the sun moves; shade is cooler than sun.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–4s | Plain sun (disc + rays, **no face**) high over land, pond, sky; heat shimmer rises; pond glitters | "The sun WARMS the land, the air, and the water." |
| 4–9s | Leafy tree appears; shady rock under it stays cool grey; open sunny rock warms to a browner glow (touch cue) | "Sunny rock: WARM. Shady rock: COOL." |
| 9–13s | Toy dinosaur (wheeled wooden toy, no eyes) slides in; sun sits LEFT; light beam reaches the toy; flat dark shadow fades in on the RIGHT | "The toy BLOCKS the light. A shadow!" |
| 13–18s | Sun glides across the sky to the RIGHT; shadow sweeps to the LEFT side of the toy | "The sun moved — the shadow jumps to the OTHER side, away from the sun." |
| Close | Three badges lit: Sun warms · Shade is cooler · Blocked light = shadow | "A shadow is always AWAY from the sun." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** sun as a plain disc with rays; shadow appearing only while the toy blocks the beam; shadow on the opposite side from the sun; shadow flat on the ground; temperature contrast tied to the tree blocking light.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** NO face on the sun (no smiley sun); no shadow drawn on the sun's side; shadow never detaches, moves on its own, or looks like a substance leaking from the toy; no eyes/faces on rocks, tree, or shadow; toy dinosaur clearly a toy (wheeled base), never a live animal; no confetti; calm motion only.
