## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: tools measure weather (thermometer = how warm, rain gauge = how much rain, windsock = wind); a recorded week of weather becomes a pictograph; and the pattern in the record helps us predict tomorrow and prepare for storms.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–14s | Garden thermometer, red column rising in sunshine; MEASURE badge starts | "The thermometer measures how warm. The tool only measures — it never makes the weather." |
| 14–28s | Rain gauge filling in gentle rain, marks visible | "The rain gauge is a measuring cup for the sky." |
| 28–42s | Windsock filling and streaming; wind streak lines | "The windsock fills up and points — which way, and how hard." |
| 42–60s | A week grid turns into a pictograph: sun pictures stack one per sunny day (4), then rain (2), cloud (1); RECORD then SEE-THE-PATTERN badges | "Record every day. Each sunny day gets one sun picture. Stacked up, the week becomes a pictograph — now you can SEE the pattern." |
| 60–80s | Dark clouds gather over a cozy house; flashlight blinks on; PREDICT & PREPARE badge | "The pattern helps you predict: a storm is probably coming. So prepare — charge the flashlight, stay in where it's safe." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** each tool paired with exactly one job; the pictograph built one picture at a time with identical, same-size pictures (one picture = one day); the prediction shown as *useful* (preparation follows it).
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** never show a tool causing weather (thermometer "heating" the day); never show the pictograph as wallpaper/decoration — it is always read/counted; never say tomorrow is unknowable (can't-know) or perfectly certain — "probably" language; storm stays calm and distant (no fear framing); no faces/eyes on suns, clouds, tools, or the flashlight; no confetti.
