## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: the ocean floor has shape (continental shelf → slope → abyssal plain → trench, with ridges and seamounts); water gets darker and colder with depth (sunlight / twilight / midnight zones) and is salty throughout; the water moves — wind makes waves, the moon makes tides, and currents (the Gulf Stream) are rivers in the sea that warm faraway shores; life fills every zone (crowded sunlit reefs, deep-dark specialists like the anglerfish); plankton is the ocean's grass at the base of every ocean food web.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–11s | Cross-section dive profile from beach to trench; small yellow submarine descends past labels: shelf, slope, abyssal plain, trench, ridge | "The floor has SHAPE: shelf → slope → abyssal plain → TRENCH — deeper than any mountain is tall. Ridges and seamounts rise from the plain." |
| 11–21s | Three stacked light bands: bright sunbeams / dim blue / pitch black; "all of it salty" | "Sunlight zone: bright, warm, crowded. Twilight: dim, colder. Midnight: pitch dark, icy. Every drop salty." |

| 30–39s | Map-style warm orange arrow sweeping up the coast and across the sea | "CURRENTS are rivers IN the sea. The Gulf Stream carries warm water and warms faraway shores. The ocean is never still." |
| 39–49s | Split scene: crowded sunlit reef above, black water below with a single anglerfish, lure pulsing | "Sunlit reefs are crowded like cities. The deep dark is NOT empty — specialists live there, bringing their own light." |
| Close | Plankton cloud → small fish → big fish arrows | "Plankton = the ocean's GRASS. Food webs keep going, all the way down." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the continuous dive (one descent ties the whole standard together); the trench as dramatically deeper than everything; darkness increasing with depth; the two different movement causes side by side (wind→waves vs moon→tides); a living, glowing thing in the blackest zone.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** never show the floor as flat sand except as the hook's wrong guess; never show the deep as empty or lifeless; no faces on non-living things (moon, waves, submarine — the sub reads clearly as a machine); don't attribute tides to wind or waves to the moon; no confetti; calm motion only.
