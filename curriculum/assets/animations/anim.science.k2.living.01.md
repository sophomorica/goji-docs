## What must the child understand after watching?

Living things show **all four** signs — grow, need food and water, move on their own, make more of their kind — and moving by itself is *not* enough.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text (read aloud) |
|------|--------|------------------------------|
| 0–15 s | Small wolf pup scales up into a grown wolf; badge 1 "GROWS" pops in | "Living things grow." |
| 15–30 s | Wolf lowers head to stream, drinks; bowl/prey icon; badge 2 "EATS & DRINKS" | "Living things need food and water." |
| 30–45 s | Wolf's legs trot across the frame under its own power; badge 3 "MOVES ITSELF" | "Living things move on their own." |
| 45–60 s | Two small pups appear beside the wolf; badge 4 "MAKES MORE" | "Living things make more of their kind." |
| 60–75 s | River enters, flowing. Four badges checked in turn: moves ✓ (gravity arrow), grows ✗, eats ✗, makes more ✗. Stamp: NOT LIVING | "The river moves — but it doesn't grow, eat, or have babies. Not living!" |

## Misconceptions to avoid reinforcing

- Don't give the river or the wind a face or eyes.
- Don't make "moves" the biggest or first badge — growth comes first so movement isn't read as the definition.
- Don't show the robot toy moving under its own power in the teach beat (it appears only in the sort).
