## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: a topic is a label ("a dog in the Yukon"); a theme is a full-sentence claim about life the book argues ("hardship strips away softness and shows what you're made of") — and the test is whether you could argue with it.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–12s | Goji seal holds up *The Call of the Wild*; a question card slides in: "What is this book about?" A tidy label stamps itself: A DOG IN THE YUKON | "A dog in the Yukon. True! But that's just the topic — the label on the box" |
| 12–35s | The label peels back; under it, three quick moments glide past (comfortable home → harsh cold → dog standing stronger). Beneath them a sentence assembles word by word: "Hardship strips away softness and shows what you're made of" | "The *theme* is what the book claims about life. Watch what the story keeps showing… then say it as a whole sentence" |
| 35–60s | Split screen. Left: TOPIC card — one or two words, flat. Right: THEME card — full sentence, and a small "Disagree?" hand rises next to it, nodding | "Here's the test: can you argue with it? 'A dog in the Yukon' — nothing to argue. 'Hardship shows what you're made of' — someone could push back. Arguable means it's a theme" |
| 60–80s | A trap card appears: "The theme is COURAGE." The seal shakes its head gently; the word slides into a sentence frame — "Courage means acting even when ___" — blanks filling | "One word is still a topic. Put it in a sentence that makes a claim — now the book can prove it" |
| Close | Seal taps the theme sentence; two small evidence tokens (story moments) attach beneath it like footings under a beam. Calm end card | "A theme stands on evidence. Go find yours — and bring two moments that prove it" |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the Goji seal as guide; the peel (topic as surface, theme underneath); a theme as a **complete sentence**; the "can you argue with it?" test acted out; the one-word trap ("courage") converted into a claim; evidence physically supporting the theme sentence.
- **Avoid:** "the theme is hidden / secret message" mystique (themes are argued, not decoded); moral-of-the-story phrasing ("the lesson is…"); more than one book example — Call of the Wild only, the lesson supplies the second novel; confetti or reward effects; text-heavy screens (one sentence on screen at a time).

## What happens next on Goji

Practice: reading session on `call-of-wild`, then journal — one theme statement (full sentence) + two proving events; then `quiz.reading.68.comp.01`.

## Brand / motion notes

Calm, clear, skippable; Pi-friendly; Goji warm seal-red/cream palette; the Goji seal is the recurring guide character. Band 6–8: the seal presents and gestures — no babyish mugging; motion is card-slides and word-assembly, not bouncing. No confetti cascades. Respect reduced-motion.
