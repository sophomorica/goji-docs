## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: a complete sentence has a naming part (a who) and a telling part (a what-happened), and a group of words missing either part is a fragment — it feels unfinished because it *is*.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–10s | Goji seal character waves. A rounded word-block slides in reading "The little cat" — and just sits there, wobbling slightly, tipped at an angle. The seal leans in, waiting. Nothing happens | "The little cat… and? AND? It can't stand up alone!" |
| 10–30s | A second block slides in: "naps in the sun." The two blocks snap together edge-to-edge with a soft, satisfying click and settle level. A period appears. The seal pats the finished sentence | "A naming part — WHO. A telling part — WHAT HAPPENED. Snap! Now it's a sentence: The little cat naps in the sun." |
| 30–50s | Second build, other direction: "chased the ball." arrives first and tips over — who chased it? Three naming parts float by ("My brother", "The puppy", "A robot"); the child-facing choice lands on "The puppy" and it snaps on | "Chased the ball… WHO chased the ball? Give it a who… snap!" |
| 50–68s | Quick contrast pair: "Birds sing." (two tiny blocks, snaps, stands proud) next to "The big brown fluffy enormous dog" (one long fancy block, wobbles, gently tips) | "Short can be a sentence — who + what-happened. Long and fancy still isn't one… if nothing happens!" |
| Close | The seal lines up the two finished sentences; the fragment blocks wait in a "still hungry" basket. Gentle end card | "A who and a what-happened. Go snap some together!" |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the Goji seal as guide; the snap moment as the *feeling* of completeness (audio click + settle); BOTH fragment kinds failing (missing telling part AND missing naming part); one short-but-complete sentence ("Birds sing.") to kill the length misconception; wobble/tip as the visual for "fragment."
- **Avoid:** the words "subject/predicate/fragment" (say naming part, telling part, "can't stand alone"); implying long = sentence or period = sentence (the fragment blocks can even wear a period and still tip over); punctuation instruction (that's `mechanics.01`); more than 3 full builds; confetti or HUD effects.

## What happens next on Goji

Practice: sentence-or-not game with the parent, then `quiz.writing.k2.grammar.01` (parent quiz) and 3 complete sentences in the journal.

## Brand / motion notes

Calm, clear, skippable; Pi-friendly; Goji warm seal-red/cream palette; the Goji seal icon is the recurring guide character across all curriculum animations. Snap sound soft, not arcade-like. No confetti cascades. Respect reduced-motion (blocks fade-place instead of sliding).
