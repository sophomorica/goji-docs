## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: a retell runs in order like a train — engine (who + what they wanted), cars (what happened), caboose (how it ended and what it teaches).

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–10s | Goji seal tries to tell "The Tortoise and the Hare" starting from the finish line. The scenes tumble in a heap | "…and the tortoise won! Wait. That's not how you tell it" |
| 10–30s | A train assembles. Engine: tortoise + hare + the race bet ("who, and what they wanted"). It pulls forward | "Every story starts with WHO wanted WHAT" |
| 30–50s | Cars couple on in order: hare sprints; hare naps; tortoise plods past. Each car is one scene | "Then… and then… and then. The middle rides in order" |
| 50–70s | Caboose: tortoise crosses the line + a little flag: "slow and steady." The whole train rolls by in one smooth pass | "The end tells how it turned out — and what it taught" |
| Close | Empty train tracks appear | "Your turn: load your story on the train" |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the failed out-of-order retell FIRST (that's the hook the lesson mirrors); one real anchor fable told correctly; the moral riding the caboose, not floating free.
- **Avoid:** more than ~3 middle cars (retells are selections, not inventories); the words "plot/climax" — train words only at this band.

## What happens next on Goji

Practice tool: read a fable in the reader, oral retell, then 3-sentence journal retell + the fable's quiz.

## Brand / motion notes

Calm, clear, skippable; Pi-friendly; seal-red/cream; Goji seal guide. No confetti. Respect reduced-motion.
