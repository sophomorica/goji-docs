## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: a multiplication fact counts equal groups; groups lined up make an array; an array read either way gives two facts (and read backwards gives division); and the whole times table is just a map of every array — much of it already known.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–12s | Goji seal watches monkeys swarm onto branches — 5 branches, 6 monkeys each. One-by-one counting starts and visibly drags, counter ticking slowly | "Counting one at a time takes forever… but wait — every branch holds the same number!" |
| 12–30s | The five 6-monkey groups pulse in turn; the fact assembles: **5 × 6** ("5 groups of 6"). Groups slide into neat rows — an array, 5 rows of 6 | "5 groups of 6. Say it: five sixes. Line them up and you can *see* it — 30!" |
| 30–48s | The array rotates 90°; nothing added, nothing lost; the fact flips to **6 × 5** | "Turn it — now it's 6 rows of 5. Same monkeys! Every fact you learn hands you its twin for free" |
| 48–62s | Same array, backwards question: 30 monkeys, 5 branches — a "?" over each row's count; **30 ÷ 5 = 6** fades in beside 5 × 6 = 30 | "Ask it backwards: 30 monkeys, 5 branches — how many on each? That's division. Same array, other direction" |
| 62–85s | Zoom out: the 12×12 times-table grid as a *map*; the 5×6 and 6×5 squares glow; then whole regions shade in — 1s, 2s, 10s, 5s, and every mirror-twin | "Here's the whole map. It looks big — but look how much lights up from facts you already own. We'll take it one table at a time" |
| Close | Goji seal plants a little flag on the map's next unshaded table; gentle end card | "One table at a time. Go climb!" |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the Goji seal as guide; equal groups *before* any symbol; the rotation (commutativity) with the count visibly unchanged; the backwards read (division seeded from the same array); the map framing with owned regions shaded — the grid as territory to claim, not a wall of 144 facts.
- **Avoid:** presenting multiplication as *only* repeated addition (show groups, not a chain of +6+6+6 as the headline); racing timers or speed pressure; more than one context (monkeys only — no mid-scene switch); the full 144 grid appearing without the shading beat (that's the intimidation shot); confetti or HUD effects.

## What happens next on Goji

Practice tools: math → times tables (tables 2–5 first, then the `multiply.02` climb), math → word problems (equal-groups items), then `quiz.math.35.multiply.01`. Division re-watch routes to math → division and `quiz.math.35.divide.01`.
