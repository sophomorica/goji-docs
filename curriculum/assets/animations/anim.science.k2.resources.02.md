## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: the things we use come from nature and are limited — take every tree and none are left, for people or for the birds; replanting works but takes many years; and reduce, reuse, recycle are three DIFFERENT helps (use less · use again · old made into new).

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–12s | Forest: stream, trees, soil; a small bird glides between trees; LIMITED badge arrives late | "Water, trees, soil, food — the things we use come from NATURE." |
| 12–28s | Every tree fades to a stump; the bird circles with nowhere to land | "But nature is not endless. Take EVERY tree — none are left. No wood for us, no home for the birds." |
| 28–42s | A sapling is planted among the stumps and grows a little | "We can replant! But a sapling takes many, MANY years. Nature does not refill instantly." |
| 42–56s | REDUCE chip: faucet turned off, one last drop | "Help 1 — REDUCE: use LESS. Faucet off while you brush." |
| 56–68s | REUSE chip: jam jar becomes a crayon cup | "Help 2 — REUSE: use it AGAIN. A jar becomes a crayon cup." |
| 68–85s | RECYCLE chip: old paper → mill → fresh paper | "Help 3 — RECYCLE: an old thing is made into a NEW thing. Three words — three different helps!" |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the bird losing its perch (actions affect wildlife, not just people); the replant beat immediately after the bare hill (hopeful, fixable); the sapling visibly SMALL next to the stumps (years, not days); each R with its own icon and one-line definition, shown separately.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** never show trees popping back overnight (instant-refill); never use "recycle" as the umbrella word for all three helps; no guilt-heavy imagery (no dying animals, no smoke-choked sky); no faces/eyes on stumps, faucet, jar, bins or any non-living thing; no confetti.
