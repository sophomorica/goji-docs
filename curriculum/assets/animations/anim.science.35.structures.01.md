## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: the leaf is a food **factory** — sunlight + carbon dioxide (from air) + water go in; sugar (the plant's food) + oxygen come out; the food is **made in the leaf, not drawn from the soil**; oxygen is a leftover, not a gift made for people; every structure on the horse (legs/hooves, flat teeth, mane/tail) and on the plant (flower, fruit, egg shell for animals) earns its keep for energy, survival, or offspring.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–9s | Big leaf as a diagram; three arrows glide in (sunlight, carbon dioxide, water), two glide out (sugar, oxygen) | "Every leaf is a food factory… sunlight + carbon dioxide + water → sugar + oxygen." |
| 9–16s | Cutaway plant: roots grip stones and drink water drops; a red ✗ over "soil = food?" | "The food does NOT come from the soil. Roots hold on and drink water — the food is made up in the leaf." |
| 16–23s | Leaf releasing bubbles that drift away; horse grazing under the tree | "Oxygen is the factory's leftover. The leaf lets it go — animals get to use it." |
| 23–33s | The black horse with callouts: long legs + hard hooves, flat grinding teeth (inset: fox's pointed teeth for contrast), swishing mane/tail | "Built for the grass: legs to run, flat teeth to grind, tail to swat flies. A fox's pointed teeth slice meat." |
| 33–40s (close) | Flower → apple cut open showing seeds → speckled egg in nest | "Flower makes seeds. Fruit carries the seed. Shell protects the chick." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the word equation as arrows with plain words; roots doing exactly two jobs (hold + drink water); the fox-teeth vs horse-teeth contrast; the fruit *containing* the seed.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** any "food pipe" from soil to leaf (plants-eat-soil); oxygen drawn as a gift aimed at a person (oxygen-made-for-us); structures presented as ornament (decoration); chemistry symbols (CO₂/O₂ formulas — words only); faces/eyes on sun, leaf, soil, or egg; gory predation.
