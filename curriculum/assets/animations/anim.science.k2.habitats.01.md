## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: a habitat is the place where an animal's needs are met; the habitat's living and nonliving parts work together; different animals fit different habitats; and habitats change over time.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–10s | Hare at home in the meadow — grass, stream, burrow, sun all in frame | "A habitat is a home where needs are MET." |
| 10–28s | Camera "tours" the meadow: grass (FOOD badge), stream (WATER), burrow (SHELTER), open hills (SPACE) — badges light one at a time | "Grass to eat. A stream to drink. A burrow to hide in. Room to run. Every need — met!" |
| 28–50s | Sun → grass → hare → fox chain: dotted arrows link them; fox watches calmly from a far hill | "The sun is not living — but sunshine grows the grass. The hare eats the grass. The fox watches the hare. Everyone needs someone, or something." |
| 50–68s | Cut to desert: cactus happy in sand and blazing sun; FOOD and WATER badges fail for the hare | "The desert meets the CACTUS's needs — but where is the hare's grass? Different animals, different homes." |
| 68–85s | Pond shrinks into a ring of cracked mud; brief flash of a forest growing back | "Habitats CHANGE. A pond can dry. A forest grows back. Then animals must find their needs a new way." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the four provisions named as *the meadow giving them* (habitat = provider); the sun explicitly called nonliving yet necessary; the desert framed as right-for-the-cactus; one change shown slowly (pond shrinking).
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** never show the hare surviving fine in the desert or ice (any-animal-anywhere); never skip the sun/water links (nonliving-doesn't-matter); no "forever unchanged" framing (habitats-never-change); no faces/eyes on sun, stream, rocks, cactus, or clouds; fox never chases or catches — gentle interdependence only; no confetti.
