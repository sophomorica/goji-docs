## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: an animal's parts each do a job that fits its life (fox ears hear, tail balances and warms, teeth eat meat, legs run); different animals have different parts but the same needs; animals are **born** with their parts — they don't choose them.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–14s | Fox in a meadow, a tiny mouse far off; ears glow and swivel; EARS badge lights | "The fox's big EARS have a job: hear tiny sounds — like a mouse squeak, far away." |
| 14–28s | Tail glows; fox curls up, tail over nose; TAIL badge | "The bushy TAIL: balance when she turns fast — and a warm wrap at night." |
| 28–40s | Muzzle/teeth glow (calm, eating pose); TEETH badge | "Sharp TEETH: eating meat." |
| 40–52s | Legs glow; fox sprints across the meadow; LEGS badge | "Strong LEGS: running fast!" |
| 52–68s | Crow lands on a branch; beak, wings, claws glow in turn | "The crow has DIFFERENT parts — beak, wings, claws — but the SAME needs: food, water, air." |
| 68–80s | A child's hand picks up an acorn | "You too! Hands grab. And nobody picks their parts — every animal is born with parts that fit its life." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** each part highlighted while its job plays out; the four part badges earned one at a time; the crow comparison landing "different parts, same needs"; the born-with-them line said explicitly.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** never show an animal wishing for / growing a part on purpose (chose-parts); no animals with human hands or eating cooked people-food (people-needs / animals-like-people); fox teeth shown calm — no bared-teeth menace at the child; no faces/eyes on the acorn, branch, or any non-living thing; no confetti.
