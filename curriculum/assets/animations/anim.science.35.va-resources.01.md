## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: a watershed is all the land that drains to one water body — the ridge decides which way a raindrop races; Virginia drains three big ways (most → Chesapeake Bay; southwest → Tennessee River → Gulf of Mexico; south-central → North Carolina sounds); Virginia's riches come in kinds — forests covering most of the state plus rich soils, minerals (coal, granite, limestone), water and the Bay's oysters and blue crabs, wildlife (deer, black bear, cardinal); and wise use means replanting and keeping what washes off streets out of the Bay.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–10s | Green ridge; two raindrops split at the crest and race down opposite slopes into two different rivers (looping) | "A WATERSHED = all the land that drains to one water body. The ridge decides the race." |
| 10–20s | Simplified Virginia shape; big arrow east to the Bay; small arrow from the southwest corner (→ Tennessee R. → Gulf of Mexico); small arrow south (→ NC sounds) | "Virginia drains three ways: most → the Chesapeake Bay · southwest → the Gulf of Mexico · south-central → the Carolina sounds." |
| 20–28s | Rolling hills blanketed in forest; a soil bank | "FORESTS cover most of Virginia — rooted in rich soil." |
| 28–36s | Three rock samples: coal, granite, limestone | "Under the ground: COAL for energy, GRANITE for building, LIMESTONE for cement." |
| 36–44s | Split: Bay water with blue crab + oysters; woods with deer, black bear, cardinal | "Living riches: the Bay's blue crabs and oysters; deer, black bear, and the cardinal." |
| Close | Sapling by a stump; street drain; stream flowing to the Bay | "Use it wisely: cut a tree → plant a tree. Street drains lead to streams — and streams lead to the Bay." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the raindrop actually racing (movement is the definition); the ridge as the divider; the three-arrow map kept radically simple; the drain→stream→Bay chain as one connected path; the replant pairing (stump + sapling).
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** never show a "water shed" building (the word-guess is a distractor only); never show poured oil or street water staying put or vanishing; never imply resources are endless (no infinite forests); no county-level map detail; no faces on raindrops, rocks, or drains; no confetti; calm motion only.
