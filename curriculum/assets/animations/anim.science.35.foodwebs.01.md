## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: energy starts at the **sun** and flows sun → producers → consumers → decomposers; the arrow in a food web follows the energy and points **TO the eater**; many crossing chains make a **web** (one prey feeds several hunters); decomposers close the loop back to the soil; niche = an organism's job; population < community < ecosystem.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–8s | Pulsing sun, arrow down to bamboo grass and banyan | "Energy starts at the sun. Plants catch it and make food — producers." |
| 8–17s | Grass → deer → tiger row; labels herbivore/carnivore | "Animals eat to get energy. Deer: herbivore. Tiger and wolves: carnivores. All consumers." |
| 17–26s | Two bold red arrows slide grass→deer, deer→tiger | "The arrow follows the ENERGY — it points TO the eater. Not at the food." |
| 26–35s | Web view: deer's arrows split to tiger AND wolf; monkey→python | "Chains cross — a WEB. The deer feeds more than one hunter. Each animal's job = its niche." |
| 35–47s (close) | Fallen leaves → mushroom + beetle → arrow into soil; inset nested rings population/community/ecosystem | "Decomposers — the cleanup crew — close the loop. All the deer: a population. All living things: a community. Add sun, rain, soil: an ecosystem." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the arrow-direction rule stated and shown twice; the deer with TWO outgoing arrows (web, not chain); the decomposer arrow returning into soil; the nested-rings levels diagram.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** arrows drawn from eater to food (arrow-at-food); the tiger as the "start" or "king battery" of the web (energy-starts-with-the-lion); any kill/gore on screen; decomposers as gross villains; faces on sun, leaves, soil, or mushrooms.
