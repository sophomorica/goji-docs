## Learning goal

After watching, the child understands: living things respond to weather and seasons; wind and water change the land slowly (canyon, sand, dunes) and fast (storm branch, flood); slow changes are still happening even though they're too slow to watch.

## Beats

| Time | Visual | On-screen text / narration |
|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 0–17s | Split summer/autumn tree; geese V crossing; leaves falling; LIVING-THINGS-ANSWER badge | "Seasons change — geese fly south, trees drop leaves, people bundle up." |
| 17–35s | Deep canyon, tiny river at the bottom; a wisp of dust off the wall; SLOW-SCULPTORS badge | "The river carved this whole canyon — a tiny bit of rock each year, for a very long time. Too slow to watch. But it never stopped." |
| 35–52s | Beach: boulder → pebbles → sand under rolling waves; dune with sand streaming off its crest | "Waves rub rock into sand. Wind piles the sand into dunes and walks them along." |
| 52–68s | Storm: branch snaps down; muddy flood ribbon moves soil; FAST-CHANGERS badge | "Some changes are fast — a storm snaps a branch in one crack; a flood moves soil in one afternoon." |
| 68–85s | Canyon and storm-struck tree side by side | "Fast or slow — wind and water never rest. The land changes too." |

## Must show / must not show

- **Must:** the canyon explicitly attributed to the river over long time; sand shown as ground-down rock; the fast/slow contrast on the same forces (water carves slowly AND floods fast); "still happening right now" language for slow change.
- **Avoid (misconceptions, brand, clutter):** no machines or shovels near any landform (someone-dug-it); never imply mountains/canyons are finished or eternal (land-never-changes); storms are not the only agent — the everyday wind/water beats must outnumber the storm beat (only-storms); storm stays calm and distant (no destroyed homes, no fear); no faces/eyes on clouds, rocks, water, dunes, or the digger; no confetti.
