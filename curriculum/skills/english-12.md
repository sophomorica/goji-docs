Verified source: VDOE *English Standards of Learning — 2024, K–12* (doe.virginia.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/53643/…, fetched 2026-08-07 via archive). Emphasis: **British literature**; technical writing. Bodies of literature for grades 10–12 are interchangeable. Same band blocker: 9–12 shelf ingest. ## Summary

Grade 12 closes the catalog with the same three-part picture as the rest of high school — reading authored-but-ingest-blocked, grammar covered, composition/research planned — plus its own flavor: **technical writing** (12.W.1.B: job descriptions, business communication) and the **two-work theme comparison** (12.RL.3.B), which together make a natural senior capstone: read two British classics, write the comparative analysis, produce the research product, and assemble the workplace portfolio. Highest-value next objectives:

1. **British-lit ingest** (12.RL.1) — the easiest shelf to fill from Gutenberg; do it in the same run as grades 9–11.
2. **Technical-writing unit** (12.W.1.B) — business letter, job description, application packet; real-world, parent-verified.
3. **Senior capstone spec** (12.RL.3 + 12.R.1) — define the two-work comparative essay + research product as the exit artifact the whole K–12 catalog aims at.
