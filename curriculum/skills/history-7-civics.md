## Summary

Civics & Economics is the catalog's best primary-source course below high school: CE.1 and CE.3 name the ratification debates and Federalist 10/51 outright, and the `federalist-papers` ingest underway puts those exact chapters on the device. The economics half (CE.11–14) is the EPF credit's rehearsal. Highest-value next steps:

1. **Three-branches unit** (CE.2/CE.5) — Kiwix articles + the shared three-branches animation + a civil-vs-criminal quiz; the structural heart of the course.
2. **Federalist 10 & 51 guided read** (CE.3) — once ingest lands: reader chapter → notebooks argument map → parent quiz; the first true primary-source chain in the catalog.
3. **Source-bias unit** (CE.9) — build the compare-two-accounts lesson once and credit it here, in English media literacy, and as the seed of the planned sourcing method.
