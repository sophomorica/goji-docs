# Grade 2 English — 2024 Virginia SOLs × Goji coverage (reading + writing pillars)

Verified source: VDOE *2024 English SOL — Understanding the Standards, Grade 2* (doe.virginia.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/59032/…, fetched 2026-08-07). Per the source, there is no 2.FFR.1 and no 2.RL.2.

| SOL | Skill (compressed) | Strand | Goji coverage | Status |
|-----|--------------------|--------|---------------|--------|
| 2.FFR.2 | Phonemic awareness: 4–5 phoneme words, digraphs, blends | Foundations | — | **off-device** mostly (parent-led) |
| 2.FFR.3 | Phonics: blends/digraphs/trigraphs; vowel teams; r-controlled; syllable types; affixes; HF words | Foundations | HF flashcards | **partial** — same phonics-strand build as Grade 1 (shared sequence, leveled) |

## Summary

Same shape as Grade 1: comprehension objectives crosswalk cleanly; phonics and grammar are the systematic gaps. Grade 2 adds one delightful hook — 2.RL.3's "compare two versions of a tale" pairs perfectly with the public-domain fairy-tale collections already in the reading list.
