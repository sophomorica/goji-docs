## Summary

Grade 5 is the **launch pad for middle school**: unlike-denominator fractions, full decimal arithmetic, order of operations, and first variables. Update 2026-08-07: the authoring debt is largely paid — every computation row now has an authored objective (`fractions.03`, `decimals.02`, `problem-solving.01`/`.02`, `place-value.02`), with 5.CE.1 **covered** outright. What remains is lessons for the planned rows plus one engineering build. Highest-value next steps:

1. **`lesson.math.35.fractions.03`** (5.CE.2) — the single most leveraged fraction build; everything in 6.CE.1 stands on it; the equivalence lesson it needs already exists.
2. **Decimals drill** (5.CE.3, with 4.NS.4) — the engineering blocker for `decimals.02`; TOOLS.md §6D row filed.
3. **Order-of-operations lesson + mental-math expression set** (5.CE.4) — cheapest full-coverage win in the band.
4. **Measurement/geometry, data, variables** (5.MG.*, 5.PS.*, 5.PFA.*) — unchanged gaps; the 5.PFA.2 variables on-ramp is the next authoring frontier.
