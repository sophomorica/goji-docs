## Summary

Grade 8 is **all gap** and is effectively **Algebra I's front porch**: 8.PFA.3–.5 are the same skills as A.F.1/A.EI.1 one notch down, and 8.PS.3 scatterplots feed A.ST.1. Whatever practice surface gets built for Algebra I (the TOOLS.md §6D module) should be scoped to start here — building Grade 8 and Algebra I as one vertical avoids paying for two builds. The pattern across 6–8 holds: computation rows are drill-mode extensions; PFA rows need the new module; MG/PS rows are teach-layer (animations + PDF + parent quiz) first. Highest-value next objectives:

1. **Linear functions vertical** (8.PFA.3 → A.F.1) — animations + graph-reading practice; the keystone of algebra readiness.
2. **Multistep equations/inequalities practice** (8.PFA.4/.5) — cap of the equation ladder started at 6.PFA.3.
3. **Consumer-math word-problem bank** (8.CE.1) — high life-value, low build cost once percents exist.
