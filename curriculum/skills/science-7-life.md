## Summary

Life Science is the course the anchor library was built for: call-of-wild's research topics (wolves, sled dogs, Yukon) already in INTEGRATION §4 carry LS.6, LS.7, and the on-ramp to LS.11 — a term-long "Wolves of the Yukon" unit hiding in the reading chain. The honest constraint is LS.1's microscope expectation: parent kit or explicit gap. Highest-value next steps:

1. **"Wolves of the Yukon" unit** (LS.6/LS.7/LS.8) — predator–prey, competition, ecosystem adaptation on the call-of-wild anchor; the flagship 6–8 science unit and the template for all later ones.
2. **Genetics unit with Punnett-square quiz bank** (LS.10) — the course's most measurable content; diagram tasks in notebooks, probability items that generate endless variants.
3. **Cycles unit** (LS.5) — nitrogen/carbon/water cycle articles + notebook cycle diagrams; decide the microscope-kit question (LS.1/LS.2) while building it.
