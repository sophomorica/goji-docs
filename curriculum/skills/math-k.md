| SOL | Skill (compressed) | Strand | Goji coverage | Status |
|-----|--------------------|--------|---------------|--------|

| K.NS.2 | Identify/represent/compare quantities to 30; read/write numerals 0–30; more/fewer/same; benchmarks 5 and 10 | Number & Number Sense | `obj.math.k2.place-value.01` (to 20) + flashcard decks | **partial** — 21–30 range + more/fewer comparison language |

## Summary

- **Pillar spine (NS + CE):** partially covered by the authored K–2 strand; extensions needed are range-widening, not new concepts.
- **MG/PS/PFA:** honest gaps — mostly parent-led or future decks/games. K is deliberately light on-device; the pillar work is what the machine drills.
