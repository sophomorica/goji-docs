## Summary

Physical Science is the most drill-compatible science course in K–8: element symbols, equation balancing, spectrum ordering, and speed/distance/time computation all fit Goji's existing deck/quiz/word-problem machinery — the anchor novels contribute little here, and that's fine. Highest-value next steps:

1. **Periodic table deck + unit** (PS.2/PS.4) — element-symbol flashcards plus a Kiwix reading spine; the single cheapest-to-build, most reusable science asset (feeds Chemistry directly).
2. **Balanced-equations quiz bank** (PS.3) — conservation-of-matter items with diagnostic distractors (unbalanced counts, wrong coefficients); pure quiz machinery, no new tools.
3. **Newton's laws + motion problems** (PS.8) — ride the math word-problem bank with d=rt-style items; this is where science and the math pillar genuinely merge.
