# Graduating from the Goji — Virginia standard diploma requirements × coverage plan

Verified source: VDOE "Standard Diploma: Graduation Requirements" (8VAC20-131-51), fetched 2026-08-07 via archive. This is the bar the whole curriculum aims at (PRACTICES.md mission bar).

## The requirement (students entering grade 9 in 2018–19+)

**At least 22 standard credits + 5 verified credits** (verified = pass course + its end-of-course SOL test or approved substitute).

| Subject area | Credits | Verified | Goji coverage plan |
|--------------|---------|----------|--------------------|
| English | 4 | 2 | English 9–12 catalogs done; reading 9–12 lessons authored (ingest-blocked); writing composition strand in progress |
| Mathematics | 3 | 1 | Algebra I + Geometry + Algebra II catalogs done (satisfies the two-course-selection rule); **all teaching = gap — HS math module build** |
| Laboratory Science | 3 (2 disciplines) | 1 | Phase 2: Kiwix-anchored units + PDF lessons; **labs are inherently partly off-device (parent-led kits)** — flag honestly |
| History & Social Sciences | 3 (VA/US History, VA/US Gov, world) | 1 | Phase 2: Kiwix units — strongest fit for the research→notebooks chain |
| Health & PE | 2 | 0 | Off-device, parent-tracked (journal records) |
| World Language / Fine Arts / CTE | 2 (≥1 fine arts or CTE) | 0 | **Polyglot** (portfolio product) is the natural world-language candidate; coding pathway can serve CTE-style credit — needs a decision |
| Economics & Personal Finance | 1 | 0 | Gap — good Kiwix/PDF unit candidate |
| Electives | 4 (2 sequential) | 0 | Coding, typing, deep-reading tracks |

**Also required (non-credit):** one AP/honors/dual-enrollment-or-CTE-credential-or-work-based item; one virtual course (the Goji itself arguably is one — verify with VDOE guidance later); first aid/CPR/AED training (off-device); "5 C's" demonstration.

## Honest reading for homeschool families

Homeschoolers in Virginia typically graduate under the home-instruction statute rather than the public-school diploma — but this table is still the **proficiency bar** the curriculum measures itself against: if every row's skills are catalogued and covered, a Goji child meets or exceeds what the diploma certifies. Legal/administrative pathway guidance is a parent-facing doc for later (not curriculum's lane).
