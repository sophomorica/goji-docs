## Summary

Grade 2's theme is change — phases, life cycles, weather, habitats — and every content row can ride either the aesop animal slot or the weather journal that K–1 established. Highest-value next steps:

1. **Habitat pass on the animal research slot** (2.4/2.5) — each fable animal now answers "what does its habitat provide, and how could it change?"; life-cycle weeks (butterfly, frog) slot in as specials.
2. **Weather journal → weather data** (2.6 + 2.1) — turn the accumulated log into pictographs/bar graphs; this is the year's SEP graphing requirement with data the child already owns.
3. **States-of-matter mini-unit** (2.3) — Kiwix read + kitchen demo + parent quiz; the first "physical science" unit of the sequence.
