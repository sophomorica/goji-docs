## Summary

Grade 10 = grade 9's machinery pointed at **world literature and formal argument**. Nothing new is needed beyond the grade 9 build list — ingest, composition strand, style-manual lesson — but two G10-specific flavors are worth naming: the argument essay with **refuted counterclaims** (10.W.1.B) and the **logical-fallacy media unit** (10.C.4: ad hominem, red herring, strawman), which teach the same skill from opposite ends. Highest-value next objectives:

1. **World-lit additions to the ingest list** (10.RL.1) — Gutenberg has ample world classics in translation; extend `reading-lists.md` before the ingest run.
2. **Argument-essay objective** (10.W.1.B) — claim → evidence → counterclaim → refutation; the compose strand's G10 milestone.
3. **Logical-fallacy unit** (10.C.4 + 10.RI.1) — one lesson serving media literacy and argument evaluation.
