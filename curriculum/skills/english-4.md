## Summary

Grade 4 keeps the Grade 3 shape (comprehension spine partial-and-working, craft/comparison gaps, composition planned) and adds three genuinely new items: **persuasive writing** (4.W.1.D), **fact vs opinion** (4.RI.1.C), and **media messages** (4.C.4 — the strand's first appearance). All three are quiz-bank/prompt-bank builds, not new tools. Highest-value next objectives:

1. **Paragraph objective** (4.W.2, shared with G3) — with a persuasive-mode prompt set for the G4 flavor.
2. **Fact-vs-opinion + main-idea quiz bank on real informational text** (4.RI.1) — unblocks both RI rows.
3. **Media messages starter lesson** (4.C.4) — small PDF + discussion prompts; opens a strand that runs to grade 12.
