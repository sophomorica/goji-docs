## Summary

Grade 7 deepens Grade 6 rather than adding strands — same coverage picture: RL and LU held by authored objectives, RI craft/comparison and composition still the debts. Distinctly G7 additions worth building for: **connotation/denotation**, **compound-complex sentences**, **persuasive-technique media analysis**, and the **memorized poem** (a lovely off-device family milestone the journal should record). Highest-value next objectives:

1. **6–8 composition objective** (7.W.2, shared with G6) — thesis→defense→conclusion; persuasive mode fits G7's emphasis.
2. **Persuasive-techniques media unit** (7.C.4) — names-the-tricks lesson; also feeds 7.RI.1 argument evaluation.
3. **Connotation/analogy vocab items** (7.RV.1) — extend the 6–8 deck plan.
