## Summary

Grade 1 is the K units one notch deeper: animals gain structure-and-function questions, weather gains seasons, and plants arrive as the year's one genuinely new topic. Highest-value next steps:

1. **Plant unit with a grown plant** (1.4) — Kiwix plant articles + parent-led seed growing; the journal growth log is the measurable check and doubles for 1.1 data practice.
2. **Animal characteristics pass on the aesop research slot** (1.5) — same chain, new question template ("what body part, what job?"); quiz items per animal.
3. **Season-tagged weather journal** (1.7) — extend the K routine; one summary entry per season closes the pattern loop.
