## Summary

**2026-08-21:** K.6 is the exemplar for the interactive lesson package format (`../LESSON_PACKAGES.md`). Remaining K gaps are queued in that doc §7.


Kindergarten science can ride almost entirely on what the K–2 chain already does: the aesop-fables animal-of-the-week research slot **is** the K.6/K.7 living-things unit, unlabeled. Highest-value next steps:

1. **Formalize "animal of the week" as the first K science unit** (K.6/K.7) — same research→notebooks→parent-quiz chain, now with a science objective ID and a 4-item quiz per animal.
2. **Daily weather journal routine** (K.9/K.10) — one journal prompt template; teaches patterns and change with zero new tooling.
3. **Parent activity pack PDF** (K.2/K.3/K.5/K.8) — shadow tracing, sorting, sense walk, push-pull play; off-device by design, journal holds the proof.
