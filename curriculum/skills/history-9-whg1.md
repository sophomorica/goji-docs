## Summary

WHI is the ancient-world grand tour, and the offline encyclopedia is genuinely strong on every row — this is the course the Kiwix delivery stance was made for. The grade 3 civilizations (Egypt, Greece, Rome, Mali, China) all return here as deliberate deep passes, so a child who ran the grade 3 units meets familiar ground with an argument-map notebook instead of a fact log. **Graduation credit:** WHI is one of the world-course options inside the History & Social Sciences ×3-credit requirement (with 1 verified credit across the area — GRADUATION.md).
