## Summary

Grade 3 is where history becomes unit-shaped and the Goji delivery model clicks: five ancient civilizations, each a two-week research→notebooks→quiz→journal cycle on offline Wikipedia — and **Egypt (3.4) is literally the worked example already written into INTEGRATION.md §4**. Highest-value next steps:

1. **Author the Egypt unit for real** (3.4) — pyramids/Nile/hieroglyphs research topics, notebooks fact log, parent quiz blueprint, "day in the life" journal prompt; this is the flagship first history unit and the template for the other four.
2. **Civilization-unit template** (3.5–3.8) — stamp the Egypt shape onto China, Greece (with the aesop tie-back), Rome, Mali; five units = the year's history spine.
3. **Continents/oceans deck** (3.2) — small, cheap, and every unit's "locate it on the map" step depends on it.
