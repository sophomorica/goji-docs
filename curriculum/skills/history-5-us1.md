## Summary

USI retells the VS story at national scale — same chain, bigger map. Two rows point beyond Kiwix: USI.7 is where the Federalist Papers first touch the catalog (device ingest already underway), and USI.9's Gettysburg Address is the natural first complete primary source a Goji child reads. Highest-value next steps:

1. **Revolution unit** (USI.5/USI.6) — colonies → grievances → war in one arc, with the Declaration preamble as the reader's primary-source moment.
2. **50-states + geography deck** (USI.1) — cheap, and every later American-history unit's map step leans on it.
3. **Civil War unit with the Gettysburg Address** (USI.9) — research→notebooks for the history, reader for the 272 words; first history unit that ends in a real document rather than an article.
