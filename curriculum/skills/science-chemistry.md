## Summary

**Graduation role (GRADUATION.md):** Chemistry is the standard second discipline for the required **3 laboratory-science credits across two disciplines** (typically Biology + Chemistry or Biology + Earth Science). The honest constraint is sharpest here: CH.1 expects real chemical work with safety and emergency planning — that is strictly **off-device, parent-led with a supervised chemistry kit**, and some reactions simply won't happen at home. Goji's deliverable half is actually large: chemistry is the most mathematical science course, and naming, balancing, stoichiometry, gas laws, and pH are all problem-drill material the existing quiz/word-problem machinery handles well.

1. **Stoichiometry problem chain** (CH.3/CH.4) — the course's spine: formula → balance → mole ratio → mass, as a graded multi-step problem bank with diagnostic distractors (wrong ratio, unbalanced start).
2. **Periodic trends unit** (CH.2) — builds directly on the PS.4 deck; trends, configurations, and isotope arithmetic are all cheaply quizzable.
3. **Parent chemistry-kit decision** (CH.1) — pick a reputable home kit and map each included experiment to a CH standard with a journal write-up template; anything unmappable stays an explicit lab gap.
