## Summary

Reading comprehension (1.RL.1) is the strongest crosswalk — the authored reading objectives + book-tied quiz plan hit it directly. The two big honest builds this catalog exposes: **systematic phonics** (the largest K–2 reading gap, needs its own strand) and **spelling→typing wiring** (mechanism exists, data doesn't). Both belong on the next-strand shortlist.
