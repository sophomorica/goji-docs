# Grade 3 English — 2024 Virginia SOLs × Goji coverage (reading + writing pillars)

Verified source: VDOE *English Standards of Learning — 2024, K–12* (doe.virginia.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/53643/…, fetched 2026-08-07 via archive). Per the source: 3.FFR.1 points to Kindergarten (print concepts), 3.FFR.2 to K–2 (phonemic awareness), and Examining Media Messages (C.4) begins in grade 4. Genre focus: folklore and tall tales.

## Summary

Grade 3 English pivots from *learning to read* to *reading to learn*, and the catalog shows the spine holding: the authored 3–5 comprehension objectives land direct hits on RL.1/RI.1, and the 3–5 grammar strand covers LU cleanly. The honest holes are **informational text sourcing** (RI rows keep landing on "needs real nonfiction — Kiwix"), **craft/comparison reading** (RL.2/RL.3), and the **paragraph** (3.W.2 — the first composition milestone with nothing behind it). Highest-value next objectives:

1. **Paragraph-writing objective + guided template** (3.W.2) — first `obj.writing.35.compose.01`; the composition stub's natural starting point.
2. **Informational text pipeline** (3.RI.1/.2) — curate simple Kiwix/PDF articles + book-tied quiz banks for `comp.01`/`info.01`.
3. **Affix/homophone decks** (3.FFR.3 + 3.RV.1) — cheap flashcard wins that close two partials.
