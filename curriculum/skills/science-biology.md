## Summary

**Graduation role (GRADUATION.md):** Biology is normally the first of the required **3 laboratory-science credits, which must span two disciplines** — so this course plus one physical-science course is the minimum path. The honest constraint: Goji can deliver the content, diagrams, quizzes, and written explanations, but a *laboratory* credit requires real bench work — microscopy, dissection or culture kits — which is **off-device, parent-led with purchased kits**, recorded in notebooks/journal. Flag this to the parent early; the write-up chain is ready even though the bench is not ours.

1. **Build Biology as the LS sequel** — every BIO content row upgrades an LS unit (cells, genetics, evolution, ecology); author them as one continuous 7th-grade→HS spine rather than a fresh course.
2. **Genetics quiz machinery** (BIO.5) — Punnett squares, pedigrees, and protein-synthesis sequencing items are the most measurable HS biology content; extend the LS.10 bank.
3. **Define the parent lab-kit list** (BIO.1) — microscope + slide set + one culture/dissection kit, with journal write-up templates per lab; this is what makes the credit honestly "laboratory."
