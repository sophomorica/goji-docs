## Summary

Grade 3 is where the anchor synergy gets strong: the jungle-book research topics already in INTEGRATION §4 (wolf packs, tigers, mongoose, Indian jungle) are precisely the 3.4/3.5 adaptations-and-ecosystems content — a science unit hiding inside the reading chain. Highest-value next steps:

1. **"Jungle survival" unit** (3.4/3.5) — adaptations + ecosystem structure using the anchor's own animals and setting; research→notebooks→quiz, journal claim-plus-proof entries; the flagship first science unit for 3–5.
2. **Water cycle mini-unit** (3.7) — Kiwix read + labeled-diagram journal task + quiz; brief a science animation for the cycle when the animation lane opens.
3. **Kitchen dissolving investigation** (3.3 + 3.1) — the year's designated at-home investigation: predict, test hot vs cold, record in journal — covers the variable-change SEP requirement honestly.
