## Summary

Grade 8 caps the middle band: the authored spine still holds RL/LU, and everything new is about **argument** — evaluating it in reading (8.RI.1), building it with counterclaims in writing (8.W.1/.2), naming its manipulation in media (8.C.4), and citing it properly in research (MLA/APA). One integrated "argument unit" would move four rows at once. Highest-value next objectives:

1. **Argument unit** (8.RI.1 + 8.W.2 + 8.C.4) — trace/evaluate an argument, then write one with a counterclaim; the single highest-leverage 6–8 build.
2. **MLA/APA citation lesson** (8.R.1) — small, concrete, reused through grade 12.
3. **Literary-device quiz bank** (8.RL.2 — foreshadowing/symbolism/irony) on the existing novel shelf.
