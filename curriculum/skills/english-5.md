## Summary

Grade 5 closes the 3–5 band: the same three authored strands (comprehension, vocab, grammar) keep every core row at partial-or-better, and the same three debts (composition, informational texts, craft/comparison reading) are now due before grade 6 — where FFR/FFW scaffolds end and text complexity jumps to the 6–8 band. 2. **Greek/Latin roots deck** (5.RV.1) — the vocabulary multiplier for all of 6–12.
3. **Multi-paragraph composition path** (5.W.1/.2) — finish the 3–5 paragraph objective at its ceiling; grade 6 opens with thesis statements.
