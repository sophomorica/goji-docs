## Summary

Grade 11 introduces the catalog's first **explicitly real-world rows**: completing job/college applications (11.RI.1.A), writing about personal qualifications (11.W.1.C), and writing to a workplace standard (11.W.3.D). These are genuinely parent-partnered artifacts — the device's role is to hold the drafts (writing/notebooks) and the record, which fits the off-device honesty rule. The academic core stays on the shared 9–12 build list (ingest, composition, style manual). Highest-value next objectives:

1. **American-lit ingest list** (11.RL.1) — Gutenberg is deepest precisely here (Twain, Hawthorne, Poe, Douglass, Whitman); highest-yield shelf addition.
2. **Analytical-essay objective** (11.W.1.B) — interpret-evidence-across-sources; the compose strand's G11 milestone.
3. **Applications + qualifications unit** (11.RI.1.A + 11.W.1.C) — a short real-world writing unit with parent sign-off; cheap and unusually valuable.
