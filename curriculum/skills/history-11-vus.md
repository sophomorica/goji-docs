## Summary

VUS is the graduation catalog's center of gravity: the **VA/US History credit** inside the History & Social Sciences ×3 requirement, with its end-of-course SOL test the usual **verified credit** for the area (GRADUATION.md). It is also the sourcing method's proving ground — every era pairs a Kiwix narrative with a Gutenberg-reachable primary source (Common Sense, the Declaration, the Federalist, Douglass's Narrative, the Gettysburg Address).
