## Summary

Grade 7 still has **no ready lessons of its own**, but the 6–8 first slice (2026-08-12) opened the shared ladder: ratios/unit rates feed 7.CE.2 (`ratios.03` planned), and one-step equations feed 7.PFA.3 (`equations.02` planned). Remaining Grade 7 is still gap (rationals, y = mx, geometry, probability). Highest-value next objectives:

2. **Two-step equation practice** (7.PFA.3) — `equations.02` lesson + pre-algebra module.
3. **Probability simulator** (7.PS.1) — small build, uniquely device-native teaching win.
