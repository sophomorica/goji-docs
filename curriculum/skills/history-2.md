## Summary

Grade 2 widens from Virginia to the nation, and it is biography-dense: 2.7 + 2.10 alone name ~30 people, exactly the shape the K–2 research-spice slot digests one at a time. Highest-value next steps:

1. **Changemaker rotation** (2.7/2.10) — continue the biography-a-week chain with the revolutionaries and the three changemaker categories; a year of research spice is already listed in the standard itself.
2. **US features + landmarks deck** (2.4/2.11/2.12) — symbols deck graduates to monuments, rivers, and mountains; pairs each card with its Kiwix article.
3. **Barter-and-money mini-unit** (2.13) — Kiwix read + a family trading game (off-device) + journal; the K–3 economics strand's first real unit.
