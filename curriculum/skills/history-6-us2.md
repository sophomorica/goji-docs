## Summary

USII is the first course where an anchor book and the history catalog genuinely overlap: the call-of-wild Klondike unit already in INTEGRATION §4 **is** USII.1 — reading chain and history unit collapse into one. The rest of the course is a clean run of era units for the research→notebooks chain. Highest-value next steps:

1. **Klondike/westward unit** (USII.1) — author it once, serve both the 6–8 reading anchor and the history standard; the model case for "research is downstream of reading."
2. **WWII unit** (USII.6) — the year's biggest single standard; Kiwix depth is excellent and the quiz-blueprint surface is huge.
3. **Split USII.5 into three sub-units** — Twenties, Depression/New Deal, Jim Crow & responses — so the heat map doesn't hide a dense standard behind one row.
