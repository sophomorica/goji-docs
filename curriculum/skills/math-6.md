## Summary

Grade 6 is the top of the **middle-school cliff**: device math today ends at whole-number arithmetic drills, and every genuinely sixth-grade idea (integers, ratios, percents, equations, coordinate plane) has no surface. Two honest observations: (1) much of 6.NS/6.CE is *drill-shaped* — integer modes, fraction-op modes, and percent conversion added to existing modules would cover the computation half without a new app; (2) the PFA strand needs the **pre-algebra practice module** (numeric answers still work here — full symbolic entry isn't required until Algebra I; see math-algebra1.md and TOOLS.md §6D). Highest-value next objectives:

1. **Integer operations mode** on mental-math/addition/subtraction drills (6.NS.2 + 6.CE.2) — biggest coverage per unit of build.
2. **Fraction ×/÷ content** in the fractions module (6.CE.1), sequenced after 5.CE.2.
3. **Ratio/unit-rate teach layer** (6.PFA.1/.2) — animations + PDF lessons + parent quizzes; this is the concept the rest of 6–8 leans on.
