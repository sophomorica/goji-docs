## Summary

Grade 6 is no longer an empty cliff on the **teach/check** side for the highest-value PFA + integer rows: integers, ratios/unit rates, and one-step equations have objectives, lessons, quizzes, and animation briefs. Device practice still ends at whole-number arithmetic — partial rows stay honest until integer modes and a pre-algebra module ship. Remaining gaps: 6.NS.1/6.CE.1 (fraction-decimal-percent stack), all MG/PS, inequalities, exponents. Highest-value next steps:

1. **Integer operations mode** on ± × ÷ drills (finish 6.CE.2 coverage with volume practice).
2. **Pre-algebra practice module** (numeric answers) for 6.PFA.3 → 7.PFA.3 → 8.PFA.4.
3. **Fraction ×/÷ + percent** after 3–5 decimals.02 unblocks (decimals drill).
