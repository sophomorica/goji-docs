# Grade 5 science — 2018 Virginia SOLs × Goji coverage

Verified source: VDOE *Science Standards of Learning — 2018*, Grade 5 (doe.virginia.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/23729/…, fetched 2026-08-07 via archive). 2018 Science SOLs (current for K–Physics; 2025 expansion covers only additional HS courses). 9 standards. Delivery stance (PRACTICES.md §6): Kiwix-anchored units + PDF lessons + quizzes via the research→notebooks chain; hands-on investigations off-device, parent-led.

## Summary

Grade 5 pivots to physical science — energy in six costumes (motion, electricity, sound, light, matter, resources) — where the anchor novels help least and Kiwix + a good kit help most. Highest-value next steps:

1. **Energy spine unit** (5.2 → 5.5/5.6 → 5.9) — one continuous Kiwix unit tracing energy forms and transformations, notebook fact log per form, cumulative quiz; the year's backbone.
2. **Electricity kit** (5.4) — the single best hands-on purchase of the elementary sequence (snap circuits); parent-led, journal write-ups with circuit diagrams as the recorded proof.
3. **"Restless Earth" unit** (5.8) — volcanoes/earthquakes/rock cycle from Kiwix; reliably high-engagement, and it pre-loads Earth Science vocabulary used again in grade 6 and ES.
