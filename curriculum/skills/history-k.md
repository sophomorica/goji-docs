## Summary

Kindergarten social science is community, symbols, and first maps — most of it naturally parent-led at this age, with the Goji holding records (journal) and serving light research spice through the K–2 chain. Highest-value next steps:

1. **Symbols + holidays deck** (K.2/K.3) — one flashcard deck shared across K–2 (the same standards repeat nearly verbatim in grades 1–2); cheap, reused three years.
2. **Maps-and-globes mini-unit** (K.7) — Kiwix map/globe articles + parent quiz; first taste of the research chain on a non-animal topic.
3. **Citizenship record habit** (K.1) — a journal sign-off ritual so the off-device rows still land on the heat map honestly.
