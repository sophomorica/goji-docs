# Grade 4 — Virginia Studies (VS) — 2023 Virginia SOLs × Goji coverage

Verified source: VDOE *History and Social Science Standards of Learning — 2023* (doe.virginia.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/58926/…, April 2023, fetched 2026-08-07 via archive). In the 2023 structure grade 4 is the **Virginia Studies (VS)** course — a chronological story of Virginia. 13 numbered standards + course-wide Skills standard; from grade 4 up, standards are course-wide (no per-row strand labels). Delivery stance (PRACTICES.md §6): Kiwix-anchored units + PDF lessons + quizzes via the research→notebooks chain (INTEGRATION.md §2/§4).

## Summary

Virginia Studies is the first fully chronological course — thirteen standards that read as one story from Werowocomoco to the global economy, and every row is Kiwix-unitable with the same research→notebooks→quiz→journal chain proven at grade 3. Highest-value next steps:

1. **Jamestown unit** (VS.2/VS.3) — the year's flagship: Powhatan nations + settlement causes/effects in one three-week arc; the K/1 light passes make it familiar ground.
2. **Revolution-to-founding arc** (VS.5/VS.6) — pairs naturally and plants the founding-documents thread that CE (grade 7) and GOVT (grade 12) later harvest.
3. **Mother-of-Presidents deck** (VS.12) — the cheapest full-standard win in the course; eight biography cards with Kiwix links.
