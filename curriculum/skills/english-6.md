## Summary

Grade 6 is where the English spine proves stronger than the math one: the four authored `68` reading objectives + two grammar objectives keep RL and LU **covered**, and the on-device novel shelf actually carries the DSR row. The band's honest debts: **thesis-driven multi-paragraph writing** (6.W.2 — nothing behind it), **informational craft/comparison** (RI.2/RI.3), and **media literacy** (C.4). Highest-value next objectives:

1. **Thesis + multi-paragraph composition objective** (6.W.2) — `obj.writing.68.compose.01`; the single biggest 6–8 writing build, feeds every later grade.
2. **Argument-tracing quiz bank** (6.RI.1) — extend `info.01` with claim/evidence items on short argument texts.
3. **Figurative language + Greek/Latin roots decks** (6.RV.1) — deck-shaped, cheap, reused through grade 12.
