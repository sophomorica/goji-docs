## Summary

Grade 1 is Virginia in miniature: symbols, Jamestown, changemakers, first real maps. The biography row (1.5) is the year's best chain fit — the K–2 research habit already serves an animal a week from the fables; a Virginian a week is the same move on people. Highest-value next steps:

1. **Biography-a-week rotation** (1.5) — Kiwix biography → journal one-fact retell → parent quiz item; reuses the proven K–2 chain untouched.
2. **Map-skills strand** (1.6/1.7) — symbols + cardinal directions deck, then a parent-led draw-your-neighborhood map; Kiwix geography articles as the read.
3. **Goods vs services deck** (1.8) — a cheap sorting deck that carries the economics strand through grade 2.
