## Summary

GOVT is where the Goji's primary-source bet pays off in full: GOVT.3 names the Federalist Papers in the standard itself, and GOVT.7–9 walk Articles I–III with Publius as commentary — exactly the texts the `federalist-papers` ingest puts on the device (Constitution articles + Federalist chapters in the reader, argument maps in notebooks). **Graduation credit:** GOVT is the VA/US Government credit inside the History & Social Sciences ×3 requirement (1 verified credit across the area — GRADUATION.md); its economics rows (GOVT.13/14) interlock with the separate 1-credit EPF requirement (`econ-personal-finance.md`).
