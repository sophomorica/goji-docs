Delivery stance (PRACTICES.md §6): Kiwix-anchored units + PDF lessons + quizzes via the research→notebooks chain; hands-on investigations off-device, parent-led.

## Summary

Grade 4 has the sequence's best-known unit shapes: the solar system is the canonical Egypt-style standalone Kiwix unit (one planet per day), and the wizard-of-oz tornado topics already in INTEGRATION §4 are the 4.4 weather standard wearing a story. Highest-value next steps:

1. **Solar system unit** (4.5/4.6) — planets articles, one per research session, notebook fact log, cumulative quiz; the moon-phase journal log adds the off-device observation.
2. **"Tornado weather" unit on the Oz anchor** (4.4) — tornadoes + prairie climate reading, plus the child's own multi-year weather journal as the prediction dataset.
3. **Food-web pass on the anchor animals** (4.2/4.3) — connect wolves, tigers, horses into energy-flow diagrams in notebooks; photosynthesis article anchors the base of the web.
