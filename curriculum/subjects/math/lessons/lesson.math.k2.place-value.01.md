## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can count a group of up to 20 things and read/write the matching numeral.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | Parent asks: "How many spoons are on the table? Let's find out without guessing." Count real objects once together | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 3 | Counting means one number per object, and the last number said is *how many*. Numerals are how we write it down | flashcards (parent walks first 5 cards) | numeral↔dots deck |
| 3 | **Practice** | 8 | Flashcard run: numeral ↔ dot-count matching 0–20, shuffled, two passes | flashcards | deck `math-k2-numerals-20` |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 4 | Parent quiz: 10 items — "which numeral matches this many?" and "write/pick the number that comes next" | parent quiz | `quiz.math.k2.place-value.01` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Flashcards — deck `math-k2-numerals-20`, 2 passes
- [ ] Task 2: Parent quiz — `quiz.math.k2.place-value.01`, pass ≥ 8/10
- [ ] Task 3 (optional): Journal — "Draw or type the biggest number you counted today"

(Existing task types only: flashcards, parent quiz, journal.)

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** parent watches first 5 flashcards — is the child counting dots one-by-one or subitizing small groups?
- **Summative:** parent quiz ≥ 8/10.
- **If not yet:** shrink range to 0–10 and rerun the deck; teens (13–19) get their own short deck before retry — teens are the usual failure point.

## 5. Tools

- **Uses existing:** flashcards (parent-synced deck), parent quiz, journal.
- **Blocked on new tool:** none.
