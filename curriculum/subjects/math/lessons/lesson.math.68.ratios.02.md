## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can find a unit rate from a ratio or table, use it to answer “how many for N?,” and spot whether a simple table is proportional.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 3 | "12 pieces of eight for 3 sailors' shares — what's one sailor's share? What about 7 sailors?" Unit rate is the bridge | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 4 | Re-teach from ratio animation: unit rate = "per 1"; divide both parts so the second (or first) is 1; constant rate → proportional table (each row is a multiple of the unit rate). Non-example: a table that jumps unevenly | animation re-teach + parent demo | `anim.math.68.ratios.01` |
| 3 | **Guided practice** | 6 | Together: 15 miles in 3 hours → 5 miles per hour → 8 hours? Two more unit rates (price per item, pages per day). One table: is it proportional? Why? | journal | — |
| 4 | **Practice** | 8 | Independent: 4 unit-rate finds, 3 "how many for N," 2 proportional-or-not tables. Optional: 2 word-problem rate items | journal, word problems (rate items — interim) | — |
| 5 | **Comprehension** | 5 | Quiz ≥ 8/10; journal: one real unit-rate sentence ("At our house, we use about __ of __ per __") | parent quiz, journal | `quiz.math.68.ratios.02` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Parent quiz — `quiz.math.68.ratios.02`, pass ≥ 8/10
- [ ] Task 2: Journal — "Write one unit-rate sentence from real life (price, speed, recipes, reading). Then solve: if the rate stays the same, how much for 5 units?"
  - Checklist: Unit rate named with "per 1" language · Context is real or story-based · Extension to 5 (or another N) computed · Work shown

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** unit rate with the wrong part in the denominator (e.g. hours per mile when the question needs miles per hour) — make the child restate the question in "per one ___" before dividing.
- **Summative:** quiz ≥ 8/10 including unit-rate compute and one proportional-table judgment; journal sentence uses correct "per" language.
- **If not yet:** rebuild from equivalent-ratio tables only (`ratios.01`) for two days; unit rate is just the row where one entry is 1. Percent/proportion solve (`ratios.03`, planned) waits until unit rates are fluent.
