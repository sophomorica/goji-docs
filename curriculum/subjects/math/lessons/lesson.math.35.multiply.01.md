## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can say what a multiplication fact *means* — equal groups, or an array — and move between a story, a picture, and a fact in both directions.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 3 | The Bandar-log problem: "Monkeys swarm through the trees — 5 branches, 6 monkeys on every branch. How many monkeys? You could count them one by one… or not." Let the child feel counting drag before naming the shortcut | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 3 | Watch: equal groups snap into arrays; the array rotates (4×6 is 6×4); the times table revealed as a *map* of every array | animation (parent device preview for now) | `anim.math.35.multiply.01` |
| 3 | **Guided practice** | 5 | Parent says a fact ("3×5"), child draws the groups or the array; child says a story ("4 wolves in each of 2 hunting parties"), parent writes the fact. Swap roles twice | journal (or paper) | — |
| 4 | **Practice** | 10 | Times-tables drill on low tables (2, 3, 4, 5) — meaning first, so *say the groups* out loud on any miss; then 3–4 equal-groups items from the word-problems bank | math → times tables, math → word problems | — |
| 5 | **Comprehension** | 5 | Quiz: match pictures↔facts↔stories; journal: one equal-groups story from the jungle ("__ groups of __ makes __") | parent quiz, journal | `quiz.math.35.multiply.01` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Math — times tables drill, tables 2–5, ~20 problems
- [ ] Task 2: Math — word problems, 3–4 equal-groups items
- [ ] Task 3: Parent quiz — `quiz.math.35.multiply.01`, pass ≥ 8/10
- [ ] Task 4: Journal — "Write your own equal-groups story problem and finish with its multiplication fact. A picture is optional."
  - Checklist: A story with groups of the same size · How many groups, and how many in each · The matching multiplication fact (like 4 × 6 = 24) · Optional: a picture of your groups

(When module-scoped math tasks ship, Task 1 becomes literally "20 problems of Times Tables (2–5)" — see `MATH_MODULE_TASKS_PLAN.md`.)

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** during guided practice, listen for "4 sixes" vs "4 and 6" — a child who names the *groups* has the meaning; a child juggling two loose numbers doesn't yet.
- **Summative:** quiz ≥ 8/10 with both translation directions correct at least once.
- **If not yet:** rebuild 3 facts with physical objects (coins, blocks, dried beans) in rows — array on the table, then the fact; replay `anim.math.35.multiply.01`; retry quiz next day. Do **not** advance to fluency drilling (`multiply.02`) until meaning passes — speed on facts you can't picture is rented, not owned.
