## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can read a division problem as sharing *or* grouping, and write the complete fact family that ties it to a multiplication fact they already own.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 3 | The stable problem: "The groom has 24 carrots and 4 horses — Beauty, Ginger, Merrylegs, and Justice. How many carrots each? …Now: 24 carrots, 6 per feed bag — how many bags?" Same numbers, two different questions | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 3 | Re-watch the array beat of the multiplication animation — then read it *backwards*: 24 dots in 4 rows means 6 per row. Division isn't new; it's the same array asked from the other side. Write the full family: 4×6=24, 6×4=24, 24÷4=6, 24÷6=4 | animation (parent device preview) | `anim.math.35.multiply.01` (array beat, re-teach) |
| 3 | **Guided practice** | 5 | Parent gives one fact ("3×8=24"), child writes the other three family members; then one division ("32÷4"), child names the multiplication that answers it ("4 times *what* is 32?"). Three rounds | journal (or paper) | — |
| 4 | **Practice** | 10 | Division drill on tables matching the child's owned times tables; then 2–3 sharing/grouping items from the word-problems bank | math → division, math → word problems | — |
| 5 | **Comprehension** | 5 | Quiz: fact families + sharing-vs-grouping reads; journal: one sharing story from the stable ("__ shared among __ gives __ each") | parent quiz, journal | `quiz.math.35.divide.01` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Math — division drill, tables matching owned times tables, ~20 problems
- [ ] Task 2: Math — word problems, 2–3 sharing/grouping items
- [ ] Task 3: Parent quiz — `quiz.math.35.divide.01`, pass ≥ 8/10
- [ ] Task 4: Journal — "Write your own sharing story: something gets shared into equal groups. End with its division fact."
  - Checklist: A story where things are shared equally · The total, and how many groups (or how many each) · The matching division fact (like 12 ÷ 3 = 4)

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** during guided practice, the tell is "4 times *what*?" — a child who reaches for the multiplication fact has the link; a child counting down one by one doesn't yet.
- **Summative:** quiz ≥ 8/10 including two complete fact families.
- **If not yet:** back to objects — deal 12 beans into 3 cups (sharing), then bag 12 beans 4 at a time (grouping), and write the family for each; re-watch the array beat; retry next day. If the multiplication fact itself is missing, that's a `multiply.02` gap, not a division gap — drill the table first.
