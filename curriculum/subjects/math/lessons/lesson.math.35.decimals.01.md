## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can read, write, and compare decimals through hundredths (thousandths by extension) as place value continuing to the right of the ones — with a dollar, dimes, and pennies making it concrete.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 3 | Hand over a dollar bill and a dime: "You know what ten dimes make. So what *is* one dime, as a number? It's less than 1… but it's definitely not 0" | (off-device, real coins) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 3 | Watch: the dollar cut into 10 dimes (each 1/10 = 0.1), a dime into 10 pennies (0.01); the place-value chart grows rightward past the point; $1.34 built from 1 one, 3 tenths, 4 hundredths; the 0.5-vs-0.05 trap as 5 dimes vs 5 pennies | animation (parent device preview for now) | `anim.math.35.decimals.01` |
| 3 | **Guided practice** | 6 | With coins: parent names an amount ("62 cents"), child builds it and writes 0.62; child builds a mystery pile, parent writes it. Then compare two piles: which is more, 0.5 or 0.35? Coins settle every argument. Extend once beyond money: write "one and three tenths" (1.3) | coins, journal | — |
| 4 | **Practice** | 8 | Money-match flashcard deck (coin picture ↔ decimal ↔ fraction: dime = 0.1 = 1/10, quarter = 0.25 = 1/4); a few money sums in mental math *(partial fit — whole-number module; decimals drill is the missing tool)* | flashcards, math → mental math (partial) | — |
| 5 | **Comprehension** | 5 | Quiz incl. the 0.5-vs-0.05 trap; journal: "0.5 is more than 0.05 because ___" | parent quiz, journal | `quiz.math.35.decimals.01` |

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** the compare in guided practice — a child who says 0.35 > 0.5 "because 35 is bigger than 5" is reading decimals as whole numbers; go straight back to coins (3 dimes 5 pennies vs 5 dimes).
- **Summative:** quiz ≥ 8/10 with the 0.5-vs-0.05 trap correct, plus money-match deck 9/10.
- **If not yet:** stay physical — a week of pricing real objects around the house (child writes the tags) before returning to the quiz; replay the animation's dime-cutting beat. If place value itself (tens/hundreds side) is shaky, run `obj.math.35.place-value.01` work first.
