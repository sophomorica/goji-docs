## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can multiply and divide integers with correct signs and apply any of the four integer operations in a single-step contextual problem.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 3 | "A sled team loses 3 miles of progress each of 4 false starts. Write that as multiplication — what is the total change?" (−3)×4 forces the sign question | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 5 | Pattern table: (+)(+) = +, (+)(−) = −, (−)(−) = +. Division follows the same sign rule (it's undoing multiplication). Three worked examples, then one contextual write | parent worked examples | — |
| 3 | **Guided practice** | 6 | Child completes a 2×2 sign chart from memory; solves 6 expressions (mix × and ÷); rewrites one story as an expression and evaluates | journal | — |
| 4 | **Practice** | 8 | Independent ~10 items: pure computation + 2 single-step contexts (debt, temperature change per hour, elevation). Word-problems bank can hold interim integer items when parent-authored | journal, word problems (interim integer items) | — |
| 5 | **Comprehension** | 5 | Quiz ≥ 8/10; journal: state the ×/÷ sign rule in one sentence and give one example of each case | parent quiz, journal | `quiz.math.68.integers.03` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Journal — 10 mixed integer ×/÷ problems + 2 story problems
- [ ] Task 2: Parent quiz — `quiz.math.68.integers.03`, pass ≥ 8/10
- [ ] Task 3: Journal — "Write the sign rule for multiplying or dividing two integers. Give one example that ends positive and one that ends negative."
  - Checklist: Rule named clearly · One (+) example · One (−) example · Both examples computed correctly

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** if the child multiplies magnitudes correctly but randomizes signs, rebuild the pattern table from three known facts (3×4, 3×(−4), (−3)×(−4)) before more volume.
- **Summative:** quiz ≥ 8/10 including both sign cases for × and ÷ and one context item; journal rule is usable (not just "two negatives make a positive" without naming the operation).
- **If not yet:** stay on multiplication only for two days; re-teach division as "what times this makes that?"; return to `integers.02` if add/sub signs are still shaky.
