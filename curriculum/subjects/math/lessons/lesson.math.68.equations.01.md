## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can write and solve one-step linear equations (add/subtract/multiply/divide forms) and use the words *variable*, *coefficient*, and *solution* correctly. Official 6.PFA.3.a also names *expression* and *term* — leave those for a later pass; do not mark vocab complete.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 3 | "Tom needs 9 tickets for a prize. He already has some, and Becky gives him 4 more — now he has exactly 9. How many did he start with?" Mystery number = equation | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 5 | Watch: equation as a balanced scale; whatever you do to one side, do to the other; inverse ops undo (+/−, ×/÷); vocabulary callouts on one worked equation | animation (parent device preview) | `anim.math.68.equations.01` |
| 3 | **Guided practice** | 6 | Four types together: x + 7 = 12, x − 5 = 9, 3x = 18, x/4 = 6. Child names the inverse before computing. One write-from-story | journal | — |
| 4 | **Practice** | 8 | Independent ~10 one-step equations (mix of types; include one negative solution if `integers.02` is solid, else keep non-negative). Two story → equation → solve | journal | — |
| 5 | **Comprehension** | 5 | Quiz ≥ 8/10 all four inverse types; journal: write and solve one equation from a short Tom Sawyer-style trade story; label variable and solution | parent quiz, journal | `quiz.math.68.equations.01` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Journal — 10 one-step equations (parent set or self-made from the four types)
- [ ] Task 2: Parent quiz — `quiz.math.68.equations.01`, pass ≥ 8/10
- [ ] Task 3: Journal — "Write a short story with one unknown. Write the equation. Solve it. Label the variable and the solution."
  - Checklist: Story has one clear unknown · Equation matches the story · Correct inverse operation shown · Solution checked in the original equation · Words *variable* and *solution* used once each

(When the pre-algebra module ships, Task 1 becomes a numeric-answer equation set on device.)

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** child who subtracts the coefficient instead of dividing on 3x = 18 is still in "take away" mode — rebuild with the scale or "3 groups of x make 18."
- **Summative:** quiz ≥ 8/10 covering all four forms; journal equation checks true when the solution is substituted.
- **If not yet:** stay on +/− equations for two days; use a physical balance or paper scale drawing; replay `anim.math.68.equations.01`. Two-step equations (`equations.02`, planned) wait until one-step is automatic.
