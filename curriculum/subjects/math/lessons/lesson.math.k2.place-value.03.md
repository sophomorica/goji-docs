## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can compare any two 2-digit numbers and explain the rule: look at the tens first; only if the tens tie do the ones decide.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | "39 or 41 — which is more? But 9 is so much bigger than 1!" Sit in the confusion for a moment | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 3 | Watch: stacks of tens make the call; ones only break ties; 39 vs 41 resolved visually | animation (parent device preview for now) | `anim.math.k2.place-value.02` |
| 3 | **Practice** | 8 | Mental math comparison set; parent calls out pairs for the child to referee ("who wins: 52 or 25?") | math → mental math | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 5 | Quiz: 10 comparisons heavy on traps (reversed digits, tied tens, equal numbers); journal: "How do you know 62 > 26?" | parent quiz, journal | `quiz.math.k2.place-value.03` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Math — mental math drill (comparison set)
- [ ] Task 2: Parent quiz — `quiz.math.k2.place-value.03`, pass ≥ 8/10
- [ ] Task 3: Journal — one "how do you know" sentence

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** the referee game — wrong answers on reversed-digit pairs (52 vs 25) mean the tens-first rule hasn't landed.
- **Summative:** quiz ≥ 8/10 including both reversed-digit traps.
- **If not yet:** back to `lesson.math.k2.place-value.02` (building numbers) — comparison fails are almost always composition fails underneath.
