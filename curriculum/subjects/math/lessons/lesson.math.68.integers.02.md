## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can add and subtract integers (including simple absolute-value expressions) by treating addition as a directed move on the number line and subtraction as adding the opposite.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 3 | "Temperature starts at −4°. It drops 6°, then rises 10°. Where does it land?" Child may try all-positive arithmetic and miss the signs — good tension | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 5 | Parent demo (or re-teach beats from the number-line animation): start at a, move right for +, left for −; chip/zero-pair picture for (−3) + 3 = 0; rewrite a − b as a + (−b). Work three examples: same sign, different signs, subtract a negative | animation re-teach + parent worked examples | `anim.math.68.integers.01` |
| 3 | **Guided practice** | 7 | Side-by-side: parent gives expression, child draws the move, then writes the sum/difference. Cover: 5 + (−8), (−3) + (−4), 2 − (−5), \|−6\| + (−2). Child narrates the move before computing | journal | — |
| 4 | **Practice** | 10 | Independent set of ~12 expressions (paper or journal) mixed same-sign / different-sign / subtract-negative. No device drill yet — parent times lightly for focus, not speed | journal | — |
| 5 | **Comprehension** | 5 | Quiz ≥ 8/10; journal: "I solved (−5) + 8 by ___ and got ___" (must name a move or zero-pair idea) | parent quiz, journal | `quiz.math.68.integers.02` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Journal practice — 12 mixed integer add/sub expressions (parent provides or uses gold-style items from the quiz bank variants)
- [ ] Task 2: Parent quiz — `quiz.math.68.integers.02`, pass ≥ 8/10
- [ ] Task 3: Journal — "Explain how you solved (−5) + 8. Use the number line or zero pairs in your words."
  - Checklist: Names the starting number · Describes the move (or canceling pairs) · Gives the correct sum · One complete explanation, not just the answer

(When integer-mode addition/subtraction drills ship, Task 1 becomes "Math — integer ± drill, 20 problems at 90%.")

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** listen for "two negatives make a positive" applied to *addition* of two negatives — that myth breaks (−3)+(−4). Correct: same sign → keep sign and add magnitudes; different signs → subtract magnitudes, keep sign of larger magnitude — or just trust the line.
- **Summative:** quiz ≥ 8/10 including at least one same-sign, one different-sign, one subtract-a-negative, and one absolute-value expression.
- **If not yet:** stay on the physical number line for a week (only addition); re-teach subtract as add-the-opposite with color chips; replay number-line animation; do not advance to ×/÷ (`integers.03`) until add/sub passes.
