## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can write a ratio in three forms (a:b, a to b, a/b) and build a table of equivalent ratios that keep the same relationship.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 3 | "On the *Hispaniola*, for every 2 barrels of water the cook allows 5 hardtack packs. If they take 6 barrels, how many hardtack packs?" Counting one-by-one fails; the *relationship* is the point | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 4 | Watch: ratio as a tight pair (order matters); three writings of 2:5; table grows by multiplying both parts by 2, 3, 4 — same "recipe" | animation (parent device preview) | `anim.math.68.ratios.01` |
| 3 | **Guided practice** | 6 | Parent gives a ratio; child writes all three forms; together fill a 4-row table. Trap: reverse the order (5:2 is *not* 2:5). One fraction-form link: 2:5 as 2/5 "parts water per hardtack batch" language | journal | — |
| 4 | **Practice** | 8 | Independent: three ratios → three forms each; complete two incomplete tables; one "which pair belongs in this table?" item | journal, flashcards (optional ratio-form deck) | — |
| 5 | **Comprehension** | 5 | Quiz ≥ 8/10; journal: build a 4-row table for a treasure-share ratio of the child's choice (e.g. 3:1 gold:silver) | parent quiz, journal | `quiz.math.68.ratios.01` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Parent quiz — `quiz.math.68.ratios.01`, pass ≥ 8/10
- [ ] Task 2: Journal — "Pick a ratio from a story (crew, treasure, recipes). Write it three ways. Build a table with four equivalent pairs."
  - Checklist: Ratio in a:b, a to b, and a/b · Order preserved · Table has ≥ 4 rows · Each row multiplies both parts by the same number

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** if the child multiplies only one part of the ratio, the table is not equivalent — freeze and rebuild one row with double-both / triple-both.
- **Summative:** quiz ≥ 8/10 with form translation and one table item; journal table is consistently scaled.
- **If not yet:** re-teach with physical recipe (2 spoons cocoa : 5 spoons milk) scaled for 2 and 3 cups; replay `anim.math.68.ratios.01`; do not start unit rates (`ratios.02`) until tables pass.

## 5. Tools

- **Uses existing:** parent quiz, journal, optional flashcards.
