## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can split a shape or a small set into halves or fourths, and reject unequal cuts as unfair shares.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | Fold a paper "cookie" in half unevenly and offer the small piece. Let outrage happen — that outrage IS the lesson | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 3 | Watch: equal shares — halves as 2 fair pieces, fourths as 4; unfair cuts rejected | animation (parent device preview for now) | `anim.math.k2.fractions.01` |
| 3 | **Practice** | 10 | Fractions module: halves/fourths visual practice session | math → fractions | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 4 | Quiz: pick the fair share / name the fraction; journal: "Half means ___" | parent quiz, journal | `quiz.math.k2.fractions.01` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Math — fractions module session (accuracy ≥ 85%)
- [ ] Task 2: Parent quiz — `quiz.math.k2.fractions.01`, pass ≥ 4/5
- [ ] Task 3: Journal — one "fair share" sentence

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** the folded-paper hook — does the child demand equal pieces?
- **Summative:** module accuracy ≥ 85% + quiz ≥ 4/5.
- **If not yet:** more physical folding/cutting (paper, snacks) before returning to the screen — fair-share intuition is built in the hands first.
