## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can say what any number from 10 to 99 is made of — how many tens, how many ones — and build one from bundles.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | "Would you rather have 6 bags of 10 candies, or 16 candies?" Let them argue, don't resolve yet | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 3 | Watch: bundling loose ones into tens; 34 = 3 tens + 4 ones; teen numbers unmasked | animation (parent device preview for now) | `anim.math.k2.place-value.01` |
| 3 | **Practice** | 10 | Mental math set tuned to place value: "30 + 4", "5 tens = ?", "70 + 6"; parent may add a quick physical build with straws/coins | math → mental math | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 5 | Quiz: name tens/ones for shown numbers (incl. teens and round tens); then journal one sentence: "52 is __ tens and __ ones" | parent quiz, journal | `quiz.math.k2.place-value.02` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Math — mental math drill, ~15 problems (place-value flavored set)
- [ ] Task 2: Parent quiz — `quiz.math.k2.place-value.02`, pass ≥ 8/10
- [ ] Task 3: Journal — "Pick a number between 10 and 99. Write one sentence telling how many tens and how many ones are inside it."
  - Checklist: Your number, written as a numeral · How many tens it has · How many ones it has · One complete sentence, like '34 is 3 tens and 4 ones'

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** during the drill, parent listens for "thirty-four" vs "three-four" — naming the number correctly is the tell.
- **Summative:** quiz ≥ 8/10 with at least one teen number correct.
- **If not yet:** replay `anim.math.k2.place-value.01`, then rebuild 3 numbers with physical bundles before retrying quiz. Teens failing alone → run a teens-only mini-deck in flashcards.
