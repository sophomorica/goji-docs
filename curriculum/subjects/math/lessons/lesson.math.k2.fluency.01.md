## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson (run over several days), the child answers addition facts within 10 quickly, using doubles and make-ten pairs instead of finger-counting everything.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | "What's 5+5? …You didn't count! Some facts you just *know*. Let's grow that list" | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 3 | Watch: counting-on (start big, count up), doubles, and pairs that make ten | animation (parent device preview for now) | `anim.math.k2.fluency.01` |
| 3 | **Practice** | 10 | Addition drill, 20 problems within 0–10; goal is accuracy first, speed second | math → addition | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 3 | Drill accuracy is the check (≥ 90%); journal on final day: "Which facts do you just know now?" | math drill score, journal | drill gate |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Math — addition drill, 20 problems, target ≥ 90%
- [ ] Task 2 (final day only): Journal — "facts I just know"

(When module-scoped math tasks ship, this becomes literally "20 problems of Addition at 90%" — see `MATH_MODULE_TASKS_PLAN.md`.)

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** parent watches one drill run — lips moving / fingers out on 2+3 means still counting-all; that's fine early, flag if it persists after several sessions.
- **Summative:** ≥ 90% on 20 problems on two different days.
- **If not yet:** narrow the range (facts to 5), run doubles-only flashcards, re-watch animation. Never punish speed — accuracy gates only.

## 5. Tools

- **Uses existing:** math addition drill, flashcards (remediation), journal.
- **Blocked on new tool:** none required; module-scoped tasks (in plan) will sharpen assignment.
