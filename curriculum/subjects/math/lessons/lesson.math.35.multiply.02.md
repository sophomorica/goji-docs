## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this routine (run over weeks, one table at a time), the child recalls every multiplication fact through 12×12 without counting — the whole map from `anim.math.35.multiply.01` lit up.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | Show the times-table map with owned tables shaded: "Look what you already know — 2s, 5s, 10s… and every fact you know backwards is free (4×6 gives you 6×4). Today we take the __s" | (off-device, printed or drawn 12×12 grid) | `anim.math.35.multiply.01` (map beat, replay as needed) |
| 2 | **Teach** | 2 | For the day's table only: its one trick (9s: digits sum to 9; 4s: double the 2s; 11s: twins to 9×11; 12s: ×10 + ×2). One trick, not five | parent demo | — |
| 3 | **Practice** | 10 | Times-tables drill on the focus table, ~20 problems, accuracy before speed; stubborn facts (missed twice) go onto a mini flashcard deck | math → times tables, flashcards | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 3 | Drill score is the check (≥ 90%); shade the conquered table on the paper map. On mixed-review days: 20 problems across all owned tables | math drill score | `gate.math.35.multiply.02` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Math — times tables drill, focus table of the week, 20 problems at ≥ 90%
- [ ] Task 2 (miss days): Flashcards — stubborn-facts mini deck
- [ ] Task 3 (twice weekly): Math — times tables mixed review across owned tables, ≥ 90%

(When module-scoped math tasks ship, Task 1 becomes literally "20 problems of Times Tables (7s) at 90%" — see `MATH_MODULE_TASKS_PLAN.md`. Until then: parent assigns the drill and reads the score.)

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** watch one run per week — long pauses then a correct answer mean skip-counting from the anchor fact; that's the ladder up, fine mid-table, flag if it persists after a table is "passed."
- **Summative:** per the gate — each table 2–12 at ≥ 90% on two different days, then two mixed sessions ≥ 90%.
- **If not yet:** shrink the set (one table's worth of stubborn facts on flashcards, 5 facts max); re-anchor on the table's trick; if meaning is shaky (child can't draw 7×6), drop back to `lesson.math.35.multiply.01`. Never punish speed — accuracy gates only.

## 5. Tools

- **Uses existing:** math times-tables drill, flashcards, printed 12×12 grid (paper).
- **Blocked on new tool:** none required; module-scoped tasks (in plan) will sharpen assignment.

## 6. Authoring notes

- Gate: `gate.math.35.multiply.02` in [`../quiz-blueprints.md`](../quiz-blueprints.md) — no item bank; module accuracy is the check.

- Division twin: once a table passes here, run its division table in `obj.math.35.divide.02` — same routine, inverse recall.
