## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can compare and order fractions (denominators to 12) by reasoning — benchmarks 0, ½, 1; same denominator; same numerator — instead of guessing from the digits.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 3 | The Drink Me bottle: "Alice may drink 1/3 of the bottle or 1/4 of the bottle. She wants as much as possible — which should she pick? Most people shout 4!" Don't resolve — pour it (water in a glass) and let the wrong answer visibly lose | (off-device, a real glass helps) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 4 | Three compare tools, in order: (1) same denominator → more pieces wins (3/8 vs 5/8); (2) same numerator → *bigger pieces* win, so smaller denominator wins (2/3 vs 2/5); (3) different everything → place each against the benchmarks 0, ½, 1 (is it more or less than half?). Optional 60-second refresher first: the K–2 fair-shares animation | parent demo (fraction bars drawn or folded) | `anim.math.k2.fractions.01` (optional refresher) |
| 3 | **Guided practice** | 5 | Parent names a pair, child names the tool *before* the answer ("same numerator → bigger pieces → 2/3"). Include one mixed number vs fraction (1 1/2 vs 5/4) | journal (or paper) | — |
| 4 | **Practice** | 8 | Fractions module compare session, ≥ 85% | math → fractions | — |
| 5 | **Comprehension** | 5 | Quiz incl. the bigger-denominator trap; journal: "1/3 is more than 1/4 because ___" | parent quiz, journal | `quiz.math.35.fractions.01` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Math — fractions module compare session, accuracy ≥ 85%

- [ ] Task 3: Journal — one "which is more, and why" sentence

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** the hook — a child who picks 1/4 "because 4 is bigger" has the exact misconception this lesson exists to break; note it and watch for it in the quiz.
- **Summative:** module ≥ 85% and quiz ≥ 8/10 with the bigger-denominator trap answered correctly.
- **If not yet:** fold paper strips into thirds and fourths and physically stack them — the eye must see 1/3 beat 1/4 before the rule sticks; then rerun a short module session. If unit fractions themselves are shaky, drop back to `lesson.math.k2.fractions.01`.
