## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson (run over several days), the child subtracts within 10 by thinking "what plus this makes that?" — using the addition facts they already own.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | "9 − 4. Hard? But you know 4 + 5 = 9… so you already knew it" | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 3 | Re-watch fluency animation, focus on the fact-family scene (4+5=9 ⇒ 9−4=5, 9−5=4) | animation (parent device preview for now) | `anim.math.k2.fluency.01` (re-teach cut) |
| 3 | **Practice** | 10 | Subtraction drill, 20 problems within 0–10 | math → subtraction | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 3 | Drill accuracy ≥ 90%; journal once: write one full fact family (three numbers, four facts) | math drill score, journal | drill gate |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Math — subtraction drill, 20 problems, target ≥ 90%
- [ ] Task 2 (once): Journal — one fact family, e.g. 3, 7, 10

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** ask mid-drill "how did you get 8 − 3?" — "I counted down" is okay, "because 3 + 5 = 8" is the destination.
- **Summative:** ≥ 90% on 20 problems on two different days.
- **If not yet:** return to `lesson.math.k2.fluency.01` — subtraction struggles within 10 are addition-fluency gaps wearing a costume.

- **Blocked on new tool:** none.
