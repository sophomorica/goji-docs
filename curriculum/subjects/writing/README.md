# Subject: Writing

**Pillar:** yes — *learn how to think* (ideas out; structure mind) — see [`../../VISION.md`](../../VISION.md) §2  
**Device homes:** `writing`, `journal`, `notebooks`  
**Role:** Teach writing craft over time; also **comprehension checks** (retell, explain, short response) after reading/math teach-practice

Writing is not “optional enrichment.” It is a **pillar**. Early grades may still use journal/writing mainly as **checks** while craft units are designed; both are valid.

## Pedagogy note

Teach → practice → comprehension often ends in **writing**: retell, justify a math answer, summarize a Gutenberg chapter, reflect in journal.
