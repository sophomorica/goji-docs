## Composition strand (`compose`) — the reading→writing bridge

**Gate rule for this whole strand** (PARENT_STANDING.md §2): composition is **parent-judged against the stated checklist — never auto-scored**. Word-count floors ride synced word counts — attested for **journal** today (PARENT_STANDING.md §2); when a lesson uses the writing app and its counts don't sync yet, the gated piece goes via journal (WRITING_LOOP_SPEC §3 manual mode). The checklist judgment is the parent's; the app only shows evidence. Manual-mode prompts live in [`prompt-banks/`](./prompt-banks/) (one file per band); the same entries are the fuel format for the Grok loop later (`../../../WRITING_LOOP_SPEC.md`).
