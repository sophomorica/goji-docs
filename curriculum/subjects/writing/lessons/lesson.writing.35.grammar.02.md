---

## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can join two sentences into a compound with a comma + conjunction (and, but, or, so), and can spot and repair a run-on or comma splice.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 3 | Parent reads a run-on version of a line from the child's book in one breath, no pauses: "Black Beauty ran through the night the rain poured down the master held on." Child gasps for the horse. Where should the breaks go? | (off-device, `black-beauty`) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 5 | Two complete sentences can be joined — but joining costs punctuation: **comma + joining word** (and, but, or, so). Each joiner means something: *and* adds, *but* turns, *or* offers, *so* explains. Then name the two crimes: run-on (no junction at all) and comma splice (comma trying to do a conjunction's job alone) | (off-device) | — |
| 3 | **Practice** | 8 | Repair shop: parent writes 5 broken items (run-ons and splices, some pulled from the current book and deliberately broken) plus 2 that are *already correct* — the child must fix the broken and defend the sound. Then build: child joins pairs of short sentences choosing the joiner that fits the meaning | (off-device) | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 6 | Quiz: fix the run-on / pick the true compound / choose the right conjunction; then journal: a short entry that uses 3 compound sentences (child underlines each junction) | parent quiz, journal | `quiz.writing.35.grammar.02` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Parent quiz — `quiz.writing.35.grammar.02`, pass ≥ 8/10
- [ ] Task 2: Journal — one entry with 3 compound sentences, junctions underlined
- [ ] Task 3: Reading — find 2 compound sentences in the current book and name the joiner

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** in the repair shop, does the child pick the joiner by **meaning** (but for contrast, so for cause), not just default to *and*?
- **Summative:** quiz ≥ 8/10 + the 3-compound journal entry (per `obj.writing.35.grammar.02`).
- **If not yet:** go auditory — run-ons are easier to *hear* than see at this age. Parent reads pairs (correct compound vs run-on) aloud with honest breathing; child calls which is broken. Then rebuild with physical sentence strips: two strips + a comma card + a conjunction card make a train coupling.

## 5. Tools

- **Uses existing:** parent quiz, journal, reader.
- **Blocked on new tool:** nothing hard-blocked. (A fix-the-sentence interactive on device would carry this whole strand well — noted for TOOLS.md when the writing vertical is prioritized; until then the parent quiz is the machine-checked surface.)

## 6. Authoring notes

- Prereq: `lesson.writing.35.grammar.01` (must identify conjunctions before wielding them).
- Quiz blueprint: `quiz.writing.35.grammar.02` in [`../quiz-blueprints.md`](../quiz-blueprints.md).
- Book sampling: break real lines from `black-beauty` / `wizard-of-oz` for repair items — fixing an author's (sabotaged) sentence is more satisfying than fixing a worksheet's.
- Misconception to watch: "comma = pause" — kids who punctuate by breath produce splices forever. Teach the comma as **structure** (it needs its conjunction partner to join sentences), not as breathing.
