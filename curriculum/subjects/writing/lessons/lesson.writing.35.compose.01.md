## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can build a paragraph that works like a machine: a topic sentence that states one idea, two or more details that feed it, and a closer that seals it.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 3 | Parent reads a "broken machine" paragraph aloud: topic sentence about Mowgli being brave, then a detail about wolves, a detail about what's for dinner, a detail about bicycles. Child hunts the parts that don't feed the machine | (off-device, current anchor) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 6 | **Parent models live** (animation: none — modeled live): build one paragraph about the current anchor chapter, thinking aloud. Topic sentence = **one claim** ("Shere Khan gets other animals to do his work"). Each detail must pass the feed test: *does this feed the topic sentence?* Closer says the idea again in new clothes — no "In conclusion," no copy-paste | (off-device, current anchor) | none — modeled live |
| 3 | **Practice** | 8 | Guided build: parent supplies a topic sentence about the chapter just read; child hunts 2 details **from the book** that feed it, says the closer aloud, then writes the paragraph. Parent runs the feed test on each detail with them | writing, reader | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 8 | Solo paragraph from the prompt bank, on the current anchor, ≥ 80 words. Parent reviews against the checklist and signs off | writing | — |

## 4. Checks

- **Parent checklist (the gate):** □ topic sentence states **one** idea · □ ≥ 2 details, each passes the feed test · □ closer echoes the idea without copying the topic sentence · □ word floor ≥ 80 (writing app counts it).
- **Formative:** in the guided build, can the child *reject* a tempting-but-off-topic detail? Rejecting is the skill.
- **Summative (per `obj.writing.35.compose.01`):** checklist passes on 2 paragraphs, different days.
- **If not yet:** feed test failing → play it as a card sort first: parent writes 5 details on slips (3 feeders, 2 strays) for a given topic sentence; child sorts before any writing. Topic sentence too broad ("The Jungle Book is good") → practice shrinking: "good *how*? Pick one way."

## 6. Authoring notes

- Anchor: **the current 3–5 anchor** (rotation `jungle-book` → `black-beauty` → `alice-wonderland` → `wizard-of-oz`, INTEGRATION.md §1) — the modeled examples above use `jungle-book`; swap the claim to whatever chapter the child is actually in. A paragraph about nothing-in-particular is a defect.
- Prereq: `lesson.writing.35.grammar.02` — compound sentences give the paragraph its joints.
- Prompt bank: [`../prompt-banks/35.md`](../prompt-banks/35.md); this lesson's checklist is the rubric line those prompts carry.
- Misconception to watch: "more sentences = better paragraph." The machine teaches the opposite — a stray detail *weakens* it. Length comes from elaborating feeders, not adding strays.
