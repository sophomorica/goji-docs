## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can punctuate spoken words in a story (quotation marks, comma before the tag, end mark inside) and place commas in lists and in the greeting/closing of a letter.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 3 | Open the current book (`peter-pan` or `wizard-of-oz`) at any talking scene. Who's speaking? How do you *know*? The quotation marks are stage directions the child has been reading for years without noticing | reader | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 5 | Three moves: (a) dialogue — quotation marks hug **exactly the spoken words**, a comma links the tag ("said Dorothy"), and the end mark lives *inside* the quotes; (b) lists — commas between items, and-before-the-last; (c) letters — comma after the greeting (Dear Anna,) and the closing (Love, Sam). One worked example of each, from the book where possible | (off-device, reader) | — |
| 3 | **Practice** | 8 | Strip-and-restore: parent copies 3 dialogue lines from the book with ALL punctuation removed; child restores them, then checks against the printed page — the book is the answer key. Then the child writes one 3-item list sentence and one 3-line letter to a family member | (off-device) | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 6 | Quiz: pick the correctly punctuated dialogue / place the commas; then writing app: a 4-line mini-scene of two characters talking | parent quiz, writing | quiz blueprint with lesson (below) |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Parent quiz — `quiz.writing.35.mechanics.01` (item bank below), pass ≥ 8/10
- [ ] Task 2: Writing (via journal) — "Write a 4-line dialogue scene between two characters, using the three moves: quotation marks around the spoken words, a comma linking the tag, end marks inside the quotes."
  - Checklist: 4 lines of dialogue between two speakers · Quotation marks hug exactly the spoken words · A comma links each tag (said Dorothy,) · End marks live inside the quotes · Parent checks the three moves
- [ ] Task 3: Deliver the practice letter to its recipient (yes, really — mechanics with a reader attached sticks)

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** in strip-and-restore, the tell is end-mark placement — inside or outside the quotes. Self-checking against the printed book catches it without parent nagging.
- **Summative:** quiz ≥ 8/10 + a clean dialogue scene in the writing app.
- **If not yet:** more strip-and-restore with the book as answer key — it's self-correcting and low-friction. Comma-in-lists failing alone → have the child punctuate the family grocery list for a week.

## 5. Tools

- **Uses existing:** reader, parent quiz, writing app.
- **Blocked on new tool:** nothing.

## 6. Authoring notes

- Prereq: `lesson.writing.35.grammar.02`.
- Per [`../OBJECTIVES.md`](../OBJECTIVES.md), this objective's quiz ships **with the lesson** rather than in the strand blueprint file. Gold items (v1), diagnostic distractors per the standard rule:
  1. Which is punctuated correctly? → **"I can fly," said Peter.** / "I can fly." said Peter. [period where the linking comma goes] / "I can fly", said Peter. [comma outside the quotes] / I can fly, said Peter. [quotation marks missing]
  2. Which is punctuated correctly? → **Dorothy asked, "Where is Kansas?"** / Dorothy asked, "Where is Kansas"? [end mark outside quotes] / Dorothy asked "Where is Kansas?" [missing comma before quote] / Dorothy asked, Where is Kansas? [quotation marks missing]
  3. Fix the list: "We packed apples bread and cheese." → **We packed apples, bread, and cheese.** / We packed apples, bread and, cheese. [comma glued to *and*] / We packed, apples, bread, and cheese. [comma before the list starts] / We packed apples bread, and cheese. [first separator missing]
  4. Which letter greeting is correct? → **Dear Grandma,** / Dear Grandma. [period for greeting comma] / Dear, Grandma [comma after *Dear*] / dear Grandma, [greeting not capitalized]
  5. Where do the quotation marks go? "Wendy said that she was tired." → **nowhere — these aren't her exact words** / around *she was tired* [quoting reported speech] / around the whole sentence [quotes wrap everything] / around *Wendy said* [quotes the tag, not the speech]
- Pass rule: ≥ 4/5 on gold; generated variants keep the same five error types.
