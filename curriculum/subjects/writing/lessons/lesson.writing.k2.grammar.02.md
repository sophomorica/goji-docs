## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can point to the noun and the verb in a simple sentence and pick the verb form that matches the subject — one dog **runs**, two dogs **run**.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | Parent says: "One dog runs. Two dogs run." Why did *runs* lose its s when the dogs gained one? Let the child guess — the puzzle sticks better than the rule | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 4 | Connect to last lesson: the naming part is built on a **noun** (a person, animal, place, or thing), the telling part is built on a **verb** (what it does). Then the match rule, said as a chant: "one thing — verb wears an s; more than one — no s." Test the chant on 4–5 examples out loud | (off-device) | — |
| 3 | **Practice** | 8 | Two rounds: (a) noun/verb sort in flashcards — is this word a thing or a doing? (b) match game out loud: parent gives a subject, child finishes with the right verb form ("The cats…", "My friend…"). Sample real sentences from Aesop (`aesop-fables`): find the noun and verb in "The fox jumps." | flashcards, reader | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 5 | Quiz: pick the noun / pick the verb / pick the matching verb form | parent quiz | `quiz.writing.k2.grammar.02` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Flashcards — noun/verb sort deck, one pass
- [ ] Task 2: Parent quiz — `quiz.writing.k2.grammar.02`, pass ≥ 8/10
- [ ] Task 3: Reading — find 2 noun-verb pairs in today's Aesop fable, say them to the parent

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** during the match game, listen for self-correction ("two dogs runs— run!") — the wince means the ear is learning.
- **Summative:** quiz ≥ 8/10, including at least one item each direction (identify + match).
- **If not yet:** the ear before the eye — parent reads pairs aloud ("the bird sings / the birds sings") and the child says which sounds wrong. Most K–2 kids hear agreement before they can explain it; build from the ear. Then retry the quiz.

## 5. Tools

- **Uses existing:** flashcards (noun/verb sort deck — seed content needed, see below), parent quiz, reader.
- **Blocked on new tool:** nothing hard-blocked; a synced noun/verb starter deck must be authored for the flashcard app.

## 6. Authoring notes

- Prereq: `lesson.writing.k2.grammar.01` (naming/telling parts — nouns and verbs are introduced as what those parts are *made of*).
- Quiz blueprint: `quiz.writing.k2.grammar.02` in [`../quiz-blueprints.md`](../quiz-blueprints.md).
- Seed content needed: flashcard deck `nouns-verbs-k2` (~30 cards, one word per card, answer = noun/verb).
- Misconception to watch: the s migrates — kids who learn "plural adds s" over-apply it to verbs ("two dogs runs"). The chant is worded to fight exactly this.
