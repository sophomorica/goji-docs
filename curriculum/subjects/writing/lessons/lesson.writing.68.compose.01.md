## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can turn a notebook of research facts into a multi-paragraph expository piece — intro, body paragraphs that each carry one idea, conclusion — where every fact traces back to the notes.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 3 | Parent opens the child's research notebook (e.g. the Klondike gold rush notes from `call-of-wild` weeks): "You know maybe twenty facts nobody at dinner knows. Facts in a notebook are private. An essay is how they get out." | notebooks | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 8 | **Parent models live** (animation: none — modeled live): sort the notebook facts into 2–3 piles aloud — each pile is one idea, each idea is one body paragraph (the paragraph machine, scaled up). Draft the intro last-but-shown-first: name the topic, promise the roadmap. Rule stated plainly: **if a fact isn't in the notes, it doesn't go in the essay** — "I'm pretty sure" is not a source | notebooks | none — modeled live |
| 3 | **Practice** | 12 | Guided: child sorts their own notes into idea-piles inside the notebook (headers as pile labels), writes one body paragraph with the parent running the feed test, drafts the intro's roadmap sentence together | notebooks, writing | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 20 | Solo (day 2 works well): full piece, ≥ 250 words, from a prompt-bank topic the child has actual notes on. Parent reviews against checklist, spot-checking 2 facts back to the notebook | writing, notebooks | — |

## 4. Checks

- **Parent checklist (the gate):** □ intro names the topic and promises the parts · □ each body ¶ carries **one** idea · □ facts traceable to the notebook (spot-check 2) · □ conclusion answers "so what," not just "the end" · □ ≥ 250 words.
- **Formative:** does the pile-sort produce piles, or one heap? A child who can't group facts isn't ready to draft — stay in the notebook.
- **Summative (per `obj.writing.68.compose.01`):** checklist passes on 2 pieces.
- **If not yet:** heap-not-piles → parent labels the piles and child deals the facts like cards. Facts appearing from nowhere → run "source or story?" on each claim in one paragraph; unsourced ones get cut live, which stings usefully. Structure fine but flat → revisit closer/elaboration from `lesson.writing.35.compose.01` at essay scale.
