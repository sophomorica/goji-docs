## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the learner edits prose for the errors readers actually judge — broken parallelism, dangling and misplaced modifiers, and the commonly confused words (its/it's, affect/effect, fewer/less) — in edit passages and in their own essays.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 3 | One sentence on the table: "Walking to the barn, the rain soaked my coat." Who's walking? The rain. Nobody *misunderstands* it — but every strong reader silently docks the writer. This lesson is about the errors that cost credibility, not clarity | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 8 | Three error families, each with a mechanical test: (a) **parallelism** — items in a series must share a grammatical shape ("reading, writing, and to hike" fails the read-each-item-against-the-stem test); (b) **modifiers** — an opening modifier grabs the first noun after the comma, so put the true actor there; placement changes meaning ("nearly ate the whole pie" vs "ate nearly the whole pie"); (c) **confusables** — its/it's (expand to *it is*), affect/effect (verb/noun in their common uses), fewer/less (count/mass). Tests, not vibes | (off-device) | — |
| 3 | **Practice** | 12 | Edit passages in the writing app: two paragraph-length passages seeded with all three families (adapted from on-device books with errors injected — see authoring notes). Find, fix, and label each error by family. Then trade roles: learner writes a 3-error trap paragraph for the parent to edit | writing | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 8 | Quiz: fix-the-sentence items across all three families; then the transfer step — run the three tests over one page of the learner's own current essay and log what changed | parent quiz, writing | `quiz.writing.912.grammar.02` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Parent quiz — `quiz.writing.912.grammar.02`, pass ≥ 8/10
- [ ] Task 2: Writing — edit pass on one page of own essay; fixes labeled P / M / C
- [ ] Task 3: The trap paragraph — learner authors it, parent edits it, learner grades the parent

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** in the edit passages, partial fixes are the tell — "to read, writing, and hiking" shows the learner saw parallelism but didn't carry the shape through the whole series.
- **Summative:** quiz ≥ 8/10 + the labeled self-edit; the objective's real bar is that these fixes start appearing in essays *before* the parent marks them (per `obj.writing.912.grammar.02`).
- **If not yet:** one family per week instead of three at once, applied only to the learner's own writing — confusables respond to spaced flashcard-style drilling; modifiers and parallelism respond to reading the sentence aloud with flat literalism ("so the *rain* was walking?").

## 5. Tools

- **Uses existing:** parent quiz, writing app, flashcards (optional confusables deck), reader.
- **Blocked on new tool:** nothing. Seed content needed: `confusables-912` flashcard deck (its/it's, affect/effect, fewer/less, their/there/they're, whose/who's — word in a sentence blank, pick the form).
