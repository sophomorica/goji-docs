# Prompt bank — K–2 (`wp.k2.*`)

Anchor: `aesop-fables` (the only on-device K–2 book today — see reading-lists device audit); Kiwix topics from the fable-animal pairing (INTEGRATION.md §4). Format + rules: [`README.md`](./README.md). Floors are journal word counts; all judging is the parent's, against the rubric line.

## `wp.k2.01` — Retell today's fable
- **book / topic:** `aesop-fables` (whichever fable was just read)
- **mode:** recount
- **skills:** `obj.writing.k2.compose.01` · `obj.writing.k2.grammar.01` · `obj.writing.k2.mechanics.01`
- **prompt:** "Tell what happened in today's fable in 3 sentences. First… Then… Last…"
- **rubric:** `obj.writing.k2.compose.01` → "3 complete sentences in time order, all about the fable" · `obj.writing.k2.mechanics.01` → "capital at the start, end mark at the end, every sentence"
- **min words:** 25

## `wp.k2.02` — First, then, last: my morning
- **book / topic:** `aesop-fables` unit (life recount between fables — same first/then/last frame)
- **mode:** recount
- **skills:** `obj.writing.k2.compose.01` · `obj.writing.k2.grammar.01`
- **prompt:** "Write 3 sentences about your morning, in order. If I swap two of your sentences, the story should break!"
- **rubric:** `obj.writing.k2.compose.01` → "swapping any two sentences would break the order" · `obj.writing.k2.grammar.01` → "each sentence has a naming part and a telling part"
- **min words:** 25

## `wp.k2.03` — Was the fox fair?
- **book / topic:** `aesop-fables` — The Fox and the Crow
- **mode:** opinion
- **skills:** `obj.writing.k2.compose.02` · `obj.writing.k2.grammar.01` · `obj.writing.k2.mechanics.01`
- **prompt:** "Was the fox's trick fair to the crow? Say yes or no, and tell one thing the fox did in the story."
- **rubric:** `obj.writing.k2.compose.02` → "gives an opinion AND one detail that really happens in the fable" · `obj.writing.k2.mechanics.01` → "capitals + end marks"
- **min words:** 25

## `wp.k2.04` — Ant or grasshopper?
- **book / topic:** `aesop-fables` — The Ant and the Grasshopper
- **mode:** opinion
- **skills:** `obj.writing.k2.compose.02` · `obj.writing.k2.grammar.02`
- **prompt:** "Would you rather be the ant or the grasshopper? Tell why, using one thing from the story."
- **rubric:** `obj.writing.k2.compose.02` → "choice + reason + one detail from the text (not from imagination)" · `obj.writing.k2.grammar.02` → "subjects and verbs match (the ant works, the ants work)"
- **min words:** 30
