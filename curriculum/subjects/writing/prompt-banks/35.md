## `wp.35.01` — The tiger's servants
- **book / topic:** `jungle-book`
- **mode:** informative
- **skills:** `obj.writing.35.compose.01` · `obj.writing.35.grammar.02`
- **prompt:** "Shere Khan rarely does his own dirty work. Write one paragraph proving it: topic sentence, two details from the book, closer."
- **rubric:** `obj.writing.35.compose.01` → "topic sentence states the one idea; both details feed it; closer echoes without copying" · `obj.writing.35.grammar.02` → "compound sentences joined with comma + conjunction; no run-ons"
- **min words:** 80

## `wp.35.02` — Sold again
- **book / topic:** `black-beauty`
- **mode:** analytical
- **skills:** `obj.writing.35.compose.02` · `obj.reading.35.comp.02` (co-credit: inference with evidence) · `obj.writing.35.grammar.02`
- **prompt:** "How does Beauty feel each time he is sold to a new master? Make a claim, then prove it with two details from the book — quote one line."
- **rubric:** `obj.writing.35.compose.02` → "claim about Beauty's feelings + 2 proofs, 1 quoted; says how each proof shows it" · `obj.reading.35.comp.02` → "the quoted line really is evidence of the feeling named"
