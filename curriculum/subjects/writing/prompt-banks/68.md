## `wp.68.01` — Pirates: the book vs the record
- **book / topic:** `treasure-island` + Kiwix: **piracy (real history vs the book)**
- **mode:** informative
- **skills:** `obj.writing.68.compose.01` · `obj.writing.68.grammar.01`
- **prompt:** "From your notes: how does real historical piracy differ from Treasure Island's version? Intro, two body paragraphs (one per big difference), conclusion."
- **rubric:** `obj.writing.68.compose.01` → "each body ¶ carries one difference; every fact traceable to the notebook; conclusion says why the gap exists" · `obj.writing.68.grammar.01` → "complex sentences used deliberately, clauses attached where they belong"
- **min words:** 250

## `wp.68.02` — The case of Long John Silver
