## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can take a short passage apart — word choice, sound, sentence pacing — and show exactly *how* it makes the reader feel what it makes them feel.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook (discussion) | 4 | Parent reads aloud, cold: "TRUE! —nervous —very, very dreadfully nervous I had been and am; but why will you say that I am mad?" Ask only: "What do you feel — and he hasn't even *done* anything yet. How is that possible?" | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 5 | Watch: mood is built with three tools — word choice, sound, pacing — demonstrated on Poe's own lines; mood (what the reader feels) vs tone (the narrator's attitude) distinguished | animation (parent device preview for now) | `anim.reading.912.comp.01` |
| 3 | **Practice** | 22 | Close-read one short tale ("The Tell-Tale Heart" recommended first) in the reader; annotate in notebooks with a three-color key: **words** (charged word choices), **sound** (repetition, alliteration, harsh/soft consonants), **pacing** (dashes, fragments, sentence length swings). Minimum: 6 annotations, at least one of each kind, each with a phrase saying what it *does* — "dreadfully = fear word where a neutral one would fit" | reader (Poe — needs ingest), notebooks | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 10 | Quiz on the passage (mood/tone identification + "which technique is this line using" + evidence items); then child reads their best annotation aloud and defends it — parent pushes once: "would a different word have killed the mood?" | parent quiz, notebooks | `quiz.reading.912.comp.01` |

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** annotation quality mid-practice — "this word is scary" is labeling; "this word does X to the sentence" is analysis. One is the on-ramp to the other; nudge, don't fail.
- **Summative:** annotated close reading passes parent review (all three tools represented, each annotation says what the choice *does*) + quiz ≥ 4/5.
- **If not yet:** annotations all one type → replay `anim.reading.912.comp.01`, then hunt for the missing tool only, in a single paragraph. Mood/tone confusion on the quiz → run the distinction on a neutral text ("what does the narrator feel about it vs what do *you* feel") before retrying. Analysis still stuck at labeling → parent models one annotation aloud, child mimics the sentence frame on the next line.
