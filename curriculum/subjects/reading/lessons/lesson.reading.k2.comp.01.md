## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can retell a story they just read in order — beginning, middle, end — and say what it was trying to teach.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | Parent "retells" breakfast out of order ("I washed my bowl, then I woke up, then I ate"). Child fixes it — order matters! | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 3 | Watch: a story is a train — engine (beginning: who + wants what), cars (middle: what happens), caboose (end: how it turns out + what it teaches) | animation (parent device preview for now) | `anim.reading.k2.comp.01` |
| 3 | **Practice** | 10 | Read one fable in the reader; retell it aloud to the parent using the train: "First… then… at the end…" | reader (`aesop-fables`) | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 5 | Journal: one sentence per train part (3 sentences); the fable's quiz including its order question | journal, parent quiz | `quiz.reading.k2.comp.01` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Reading — `aesop-fables`, one fable (~10 min)
- [ ] Task 2: Journal — "You just read a fable. Retell it in 3 sentences: what happened at the beginning, the middle, and the end."
  - Checklist: Sentence 1: the beginning — who was it about, what did they want? · Sentence 2: the middle — what happened? · Sentence 3: the end — how did it turn out? · Bonus: the fable's message in your own words
- [ ] Task 3: Parent quiz — that fable's check, pass ≥ 4/5

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** the oral retell — does it start with the character or jump to the ending? Jumping to the end = re-anchor with "who was it about? what did they want?"
- **Summative:** journal retell hits all 3 parts + the message, on 2 different fables.
- **If not yet:** retell with the book open (page-flip retell), then closed; picture-sequence cards for pre-writers.

## 5. Tools

- **Uses existing:** reader, journal, parent quiz.
- **Blocked on new tool:** none.
