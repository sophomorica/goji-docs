## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson (as a per-chapter habit), the child attacks unfamiliar words with context first, the lookup tool second — and keeps the best finds until they own them.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | "Alice found a bottle marked DRINK ME. You'll find words marked LOOK ME UP. Collectors get curious, not stuck" | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 3 | Word-attack order: reread the sentence → guess from context → look it up → keep it. A guess checked by lookup beats a lookup with no guess | (parent-led) | — |
| 3 | **Practice** | 15 | Read a chapter in the reader; use word lookups freely (the count syncs to the parent — curiosity is visible!); pick 5 favorite finds | reader (`alice-wonderland`), word lookups | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 5 | The 5 finds go into the chapter's flashcard deck (parent adds them); deck run to ≥ 90% over the coming days | flashcards | deck gate |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Reading — `alice-wonderland`, one chapter
- [ ] Task 2: Flashcards — deck `vocab-alice-ch<N>` (last chapter's finds), ≥ 90%
- [ ] Task 3 (unit spice): Research — one Wonderland-real thing (flamingos, hedgehogs, croquet, playing cards) on offline Wikipedia

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** watch one unknown-word moment — does the child guess before looking up? Guessing first is the skill being built.
- **Summative:** deck ≥ 90% two days running; child uses 2 collected words in a journal sentence.
- **If not yet:** parent pre-picks 3 words per chapter instead of 5 child-picked; oral sentences before written.

## 5. Tools

- **Uses existing:** reader + word lookup (count already syncs — PARENT_STANDING signal), flashcards (parent-synced decks), research, journal.
- **Blocked on new tool:** none. (Auto "lookup → deck" pipe would remove the parent copy step — worthwhile TOOLS.md §6 note, not a blocker.)

## 6. Authoring notes

- Animation: none — the word-attack routine is a habit, taught live.
- Quiz: deck gate per chapter deck.
- Integration: Alice anchors vocabulary because Carroll's precise-but-strange words ("curious," "remarkable," "venture") are exactly the collectible kind; the research topics are Wonderland's real objects, so even whimsy connects back to the world.
