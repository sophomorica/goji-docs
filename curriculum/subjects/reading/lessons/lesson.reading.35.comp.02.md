## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can say how Beauty feels or why a character acts — and quote the line that proves it — even when the book never says the feeling outright.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | Parent slumps, sighs, stares at shoes — says nothing. "How do I feel? How did you KNOW? I never said it" | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 3 | Watch: authors leave clues instead of labels; clue + what-you-know = inference; claims need a proof line | animation (parent device preview for now) | `anim.reading.35.comp.02` |
| 3 | **Practice** | 15 | Read a chapter chunk in the reader; two feelings-questions from parent, answered as claim + quoted line | reader (`black-beauty`) | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 8 | Chapter quiz (every inference item stores its evidence line); journal exit ticket: "How did Beauty feel when ___? How do you know?" | parent quiz, journal | `quiz.reading.35.comp.02` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Reading — `black-beauty`, ~20 min
- [ ] Task 2: Parent quiz — chapter check, pass ≥ 4/5
- [ ] Task 3: Journal — "From the Black Beauty chapter you just read: write one claim about a character or event, then the line from the book that proves it."
  - Checklist: One claim about today's chapter · The proof line, quoted or closely retold from the book · One sentence on how the line proves your claim
- [ ] Task 4 (unit spice): Research — "horse" / "Victorian era carriages" on offline Wikipedia; how real horses signal fear → one note in notebooks

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** claims without quotes — "show me the line" until it's reflex.
- **Summative:** ≥ 4/5 with evidence lines on two chapter chunks.
- **If not yet:** run inference on pictures first (face cards, "how do they feel, what's the clue"), then one paragraph, then chapters.
