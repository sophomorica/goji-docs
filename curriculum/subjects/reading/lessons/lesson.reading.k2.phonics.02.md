## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child reads VCe words (cape, kite, note) by spotting the silent-e and switching the vowel to its long sound.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | Write `cap`. Child reads it. Add one letter: `cape`. "One silent letter just changed the whole word. How?" | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 3 | Watch: magic-e reaches back over one consonant and wakes the vowel to say its name; minimal pairs transform | animation (parent device preview for now) | `anim.reading.k2.phonics.02` |
| 3 | **Practice** | 8 | Flashcard run: VCe deck heavy on minimal pairs (cap/cape, kit/kite, hop/hope, cut/cute) | flashcards | deck `read-k2-vce` |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 2 | Deck gate ≥ 9/10; type 5 pairs in typing app (both forms — proves the child controls the switch) | flashcards score, typing | deck gate |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Flashcards — deck `read-k2-vce`, read-aloud run
- [ ] Task 2: Typing — 5 minimal pairs

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** show `pin` then `pine` — does the child scan to the end of the word before saying it? End-scanning is the skill.
- **Summative:** ≥ 9/10 including at least 4 minimal-pair items.
- **If not yet:** back to `lesson.reading.k2.phonics.01` confidence, then re-teach with physical letter tiles — slide the `e` on and off the same word.

## 5. Tools

- **Uses existing:** flashcards, typing.
- **Blocked on new tool:** none.
