## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson (run over several days), the child can sound out any short-vowel CVC word — cat, hop, sun — instead of guessing from the picture.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | "Words are a secret code. Today you get the decoder." Write `sun` — what three sounds hide inside? | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 3 | Watch: letters make sounds, sounds blend left-to-right; c-a-t slides into "cat" | animation (parent device preview for now) | `anim.reading.k2.phonics.01` |
| 3 | **Practice** | 8 | Decodable flashcard run: CVC deck, child reads each aloud to parent, self-checks the back | flashcards | deck `read-k2-cvc` |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 2 | Deck gate: ≥ 9/10 across the run; then type 5 of the words in typing app (encode = decode's proof) | flashcards score, typing | deck gate |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Flashcards — deck `read-k2-cvc`, read-aloud run with parent nearby
- [ ] Task 2: Typing — same 5–10 words as a custom exercise

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** parent listens — is the child blending ("c…a…t → cat") or guessing whole words? Guessing on decodables means slow down, don't speed up.
- **Summative:** ≥ 9/10 on the deck two days running.
- **If not yet:** shrink to one vowel family per session (all short-a words); replay the animation; keep sessions short and daily — phonics compounds.

## 5. Tools

- **Uses existing:** flashcards (parent-synced deck), typing, parent observation.
- **Blocked on new tool:** none. (Audio-per-card would help pre-readers — noted in TOOLS.md backlog territory, not required.)
