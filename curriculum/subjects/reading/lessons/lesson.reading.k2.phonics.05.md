## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child reads diphthong and variant-vowel words (coin, boy, mouse, down, saw, moon, book) by hearing the sliding or special sound each team makes — including that `oo` has two different voices.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | "What do you say when you stub your toe? OW! What does a pirate say? Oy! Those noises are hiding inside words: d-OW-n, b-OY. Today's teams don't say a vowel's name — they make noises." | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 3 | Parent demo, one team at a time with its sound-effect anchor: **oi/oy** = "ahoy!" · **ou/ow** = "ouch!" · **au/aw** = the crow's "caw" · **oo** = moon-howl "oooo" *or* book-push "uh-oo" (good, look). | 3 | **Practice** | 8 | Flashcard run: diphthong deck grouped by team, child reads each aloud to parent, self-checks the back; end with a moon-oo vs book-oo sort of the day's `oo` cards | flashcards | deck `read-k2-diphthongs` |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 2 | Deck gate: ≥ 8/10 per pattern group with both `oo` sounds sampled; then type 5 of the words in typing app | flashcards score, typing | deck gate |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Flashcards — deck `read-k2-diphthongs`, read-aloud run with parent nearby (one or two teams per day)
- [ ] Task 2: Typing — same 5–8 words as a custom exercise

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** show `out` — does the child slide the "ouch" sound, or read it as long-o "oat"? Long-vowel substitution means the child is over-applying `.03`'s rule: re-anchor with the sound effect before each card ("this team says OUCH"). For `oo` cards, ask "moon-oo or book-oo?" *before* the read.
- **Summative:** ≥ 8/10 per pattern group (oi/oy, ou/ow, au/aw, oo), two days running, including the contrast cards (coin/cone, out/oat).
- **If not yet:** one team per session, sound-effect first ("today is OUCH day"); if the child confuses these with `.03`'s name-saying teams, run a 6-card sort — name-sayers (rain, coat) vs noise-makers (out, coin) — before the deck; `oo` confusion alone is normal, keep the moon/book sort daily until it settles.

- **Blocked on new tool:** none.
