## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child reads 2-syllable words (rabbit, tiger, acorn) by chopping them into an open or closed bite each, and reads endings (-s, -ing, -ed) as snap-on parts instead of new words.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | Write `rabbit`. "Too big for one bite? Chop it: rab / bit. You can already read BOTH bites!" Then `tiger`: chop after the i — "ti / ger. When a bite ends on its vowel, the vowel says its name." | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 3 | Parent demo with a pencil chop-line: two consonants in the middle → chop between them, first vowel stays short (rab-bit, **closed**); one consonant → chop after the vowel, it says its name (ti-ger, **open** — same long sound as `.02`). Then endings: jump → jumps → jumping → jumped; -ed's three sounds (jump**ed** /t/, call**ed** /d/, want**ed** /id/) | (off-device — animation TBD) | TBD |
| 3 | **Practice** | 8 | Flashcard run: 2-syllable deck, child draws the chop with a finger, reads bite-by-bite, then whole; ending cards read base-then-whole (jump… jumped) | flashcards | deck `read-k2-2syllable` |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 2 | Deck gate: ≥ 8/10 incl. open/closed contrasts and -ed cards; then type 3 base words with their -ing and -ed forms (typing the doubled letter in *hopping* proves the rule) | flashcards score, typing | deck gate |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Flashcards — deck `read-k2-2syllable`, read-aloud run with parent nearby (chop with a finger before each read)
- [ ] Task 2: Typing — 3 base words + their -ing/-ed forms as a custom exercise

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** show `robin` then `pilot` — does the child *try a chop* and adjust ("ro…bin? rob-in!"), or freeze at any word longer than 4 letters? Freezing means the chop move isn't automatic yet — parent draws the line for a few cards, child reads the bites. On -ed cards, listen for "want-ED" on jumped (over-pronouncing) — say the pair fast aloud together.
- **Summative:** ≥ 8/10 on the deck two days running, including at least 3 open/closed contrast cards and 3 -ed cards covering all three sounds.
- **If not yet:** off-device scissors round — write the word big on paper, child physically cuts between the syllables, reads each piece; if open-syllable long vowels are the failure (ti-ger read "tigger"), back to `lesson.reading.k2.phonics.02` — same vowel-says-its-name idea; hopping/hoping confusion goes straight to the `.02` minimal-pair tiles.

## 5. Tools

- **Uses existing:** flashcards, typing, parent observation (+ paper and scissors, off-device).
- **Blocked on new tool:** none.
