## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson (run over several days), the child reads vowel-team words (rain, sheep, goat, meat) as one long-vowel voice and bossy-r words (farm, corn, her) as one r-changed chunk — no letter-by-letter sounding through the team.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | Write `met`. Child reads it. Add one letter: `meat`. "Two vowels just teamed up. Only ONE of them gets to talk. Which one?" | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 3 | Watch: two vowels walk side by side, the first one speaks its name, the second zips its lips; then `r` grabs a vowel and changes its voice | animation (parent device preview for now) | `anim.reading.k2.phonics.03` |
| 3 | **Practice** | 8 | Flashcard run: vowel-team deck first (until it gates), then the bossy-r deck; child reads each aloud to parent, self-checks the back | flashcards | decks `read-k2-vteams`, `read-k2-bossy-r` |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 2 | Deck gate: ≥ 8/10 per pattern deck; then type 5 of the words in typing app (spelling the team = proof it's seen as a unit) | flashcards score, typing | deck gate |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Flashcards — deck `read-k2-vteams` (or `read-k2-bossy-r` once teams gate), read-aloud run with parent nearby
- [ ] Task 2: Typing — same 5–8 words as a custom exercise

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** parent listens — does the child *spot the team first* ("m…ea…t → meat") or grind through four separate letters ("m-e-a-t")? Letter-grinding on team words means point at the two vowels before each card: "team — one voice."
- **Summative:** ≥ 8/10 on each pattern deck (vowel teams; bossy-r), two days running, including the contrast cards (rain/ran, coat/cot).
- **If not yet:** shrink to one team per session (all-`ai` day, all-`ee` day); replay the animation; if long-vowel *sounds* are the problem (not the spotting), drop back to `lesson.reading.k2.phonics.02` — magic-e wakes the same vowels. For bossy-r, physical letter tiles with the `r` taped to its vowel as one un-splittable tile.

## 5. Tools

- **Uses existing:** flashcards (parent-synced decks), typing, parent observation.
- **Blocked on new tool:** none.
