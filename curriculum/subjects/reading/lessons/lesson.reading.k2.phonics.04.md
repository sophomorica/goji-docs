## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child reads digraph words (ship, chat, that, when, duck) with the two letters as *one brand-new sound*, and blend words (stop, grass, frog) with both sounds kept but glued fast.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | "Say /s/. Now say /h/. Now say them fast — /s/-/h/-/s/-/h/… Did you ever get 'sh'? You can't! When s and h hold hands, they make a sound neither one owns." | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 3 | Watch: two letter tiles fuse into ONE new-sound tile (sh, ch, th); then blend tiles stay two sounds but slide extra fast (st, gr) | animation (parent device preview for now) | `anim.reading.k2.phonics.04` |
| 3 | **Practice** | 8 | Flashcard run: digraph deck first (until it gates), then blends deck; child reads each aloud to parent, self-checks the back | flashcards | decks `read-k2-digraphs`, `read-k2-blends` |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 2 | Deck gate: ≥ 9/10 per deck incl. minimal pairs; then type 5 of the words (typing sh as two keys while saying ONE sound seals the idea) | flashcards score, typing | deck gate |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Flashcards — deck `read-k2-digraphs` (or `read-k2-blends` once digraphs gate), read-aloud run with parent nearby
- [ ] Task 2: Typing — same 5–10 words as a custom exercise

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** show `ship` — does the child say /s/-/h/-/i/-/p/ (splitting the team) or /sh/-/i/-/p/? Splitting means cover the `sh` with a finger and ask "what one sound do these two make together?" For blends the opposite check: `stop` must keep BOTH /s/ and /t/ — dropping to "top" or "sop" means slow the slide down, don't skip it.
- **Summative:** ≥ 9/10 on each deck two days running, including at least 4 minimal-pair cards (ship/sip, chat/cat, then/ten; stop/top, black/back).
- **If not yet:** one team per session (all-`sh` day); mirror game — watch your own mouth make /s/ then /sh/ (lips round!); if single-letter sounds are shaky, back to `lesson.reading.k2.phonics.01` and physical tiles with each digraph welded as one un-cuttable tile.

## 5. Tools

- **Uses existing:** flashcards, typing, parent observation (+ a mirror, off-device).
- **Blocked on new tool:** none.
