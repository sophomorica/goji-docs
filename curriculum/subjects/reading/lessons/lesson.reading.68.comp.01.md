## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child can state a novel's theme as a full-sentence claim about life — and explain why "a dog in the Yukon" is a topic, not a theme.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook (discussion) | 3 | Ask: "What is *The Call of the Wild* about?" Accept "a dog in the Yukon" — then push: "That's what it's *about*. What does it *say*? What does hardship do to Buck?" Don't resolve yet | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 4 | Watch: topic = a label, theme = a claim you could argue with; the topic→theme test applied to Call of the Wild | animation (parent device preview for now) | `anim.reading.68.comp.01` |
| 3 | **Practice** | 18 | Reading session on the assigned chapter-chunk; then journal: write ONE theme statement as a full sentence + list two events from the book that prove it. Weak form ("courage", "survival") gets bounced back: "That's a topic word — what does the book claim about it?" | reader (`call-of-wild`), journal | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 8 | Quiz: sort topic vs theme statements, pick the best-supported theme, match evidence to claim; parent reads the journal theme statement aloud and asks "could someone argue against this?" (if no — it's still a topic) | parent quiz, journal | `quiz.reading.68.comp.01` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Reading — `call-of-wild`, 20 min (current chapter-chunk)
- [ ] Task 2: Journal — one theme statement (full sentence) + two proving events
- [ ] Task 3: Parent quiz — `quiz.reading.68.comp.01`, pass ≥ 5/6

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** the hook — does the child's first answer name a topic? (Almost always yes; that's the setup, not a failure.)
- **Summative:** quiz ≥ 5/6 AND the journal theme statement passes the "could you argue with it?" test on parent review. Objective completes after the same pass on a second novel (`treasure-island`).
- **If not yet:** single-word answers ("courage") → replay `anim.reading.68.comp.01` and rebuild together: "The book says courage ___" until the blank is filled with a claim. Theme unsupported by events → reread one chapter-chunk hunting for two proving moments before rewriting.
