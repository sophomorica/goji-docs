## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson (as a running habit), the child reads a short fable aloud with accuracy and phrasing — like telling a story, not naming words.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | Parent reads two lines of the fable twice: once robot-flat, once alive. "Which one made you want more?" | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 2 | Punctuation is stage direction: periods breathe, questions rise, names get voices | (parent-led, no media) | — |
| 3 | **Practice** | 12 | Child reads one Aesop fable in the reader — first silently, then aloud to the parent; reread favorite lines with expression | reader (`aesop-fables`) | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 4 | Parent fluency judgment (gate) + the fable's retell quiz when done | parent observation, parent quiz | `quiz.reading.k2.comp.01` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Reading — `aesop-fables`, 15 minutes
- [ ] Task 2: Parent quiz — that fable's 5-item check (pass ≥ 4/5)

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** the aloud read — count stumbles per page (0–2 fine, 5+ means the text is too hard today, pick a shorter fable).
- **Summative:** gate.reading.k2.fluency.01 — parent judgment, logged minutes accumulating.
- **If not yet:** echo reading (parent reads a line, child reads it back) on the same fable — same text, lower load; decode gaps → back to phonics lessons.

## 5. Tools

- **Uses existing:** reader (Aesop is on device), parent quiz, reading minutes sync.
- **Blocked on new tool:** none. Guided-reading modes (chunking) are a later TOOLS.md idea, not needed here.

## 6. Authoring notes

- Animation: deliberately none — fluency is modeled by a live reader, not a clip.
- Quiz blueprint: `quiz.reading.k2.comp.01` (per-fable, already authored).
- Seed content needed: none — Aesop's 17 chapters are the fable supply.
