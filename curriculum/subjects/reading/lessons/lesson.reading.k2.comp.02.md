## 1. Goal (one sentence)

After this lesson, the child answers who/what/where/when questions about a fable using the text — pointing at the line that says so.

## 2. Sequence (teach → practice → comprehension)

| Step | Pattern | Minutes | Activity | Goji tool | Media |
|------|---------|---------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Hook | 2 | Parent asks an unanswerable question about the last fable ("What color was the shepherd boy's hat?"). "The book didn't say! Detectives only claim what the text shows" | (off-device) | — |
| 2 | **Teach** | 2 | Question words each hunt something: WHO → a character, WHERE → a place, WHAT → an action. Answers live in the text, not in guesses | (parent-led) | — |
| 3 | **Practice** | 10 | Read the next fable in the reader; parent asks 4 detective questions; child answers AND finds the sentence that proves it | reader (`aesop-fables`) | — |
| 4 | **Comprehension** | 5 | The fable's quiz (who/what/where items use other fables' details as distractors — real detectives can't bluff); animal fact hunt: look up the fable's animal in research app, one fact into journal | parent quiz, research, journal | `quiz.reading.k2.comp.01` |

## 3. School Day mapping (how a parent would assign today)

- [ ] Task 1: Reading — `aesop-fables`, one fable
- [ ] Task 2: Parent quiz — that fable's check, pass ≥ 4/5
- [ ] Task 3: Research + journal — one true fact about the fable's animal (wolf, fox, crow…) from offline Wikipedia

## 4. Checks

- **Formative:** the prove-it step — an answer without a pointed-at sentence is a guess, gently send them back in.
- **Summative:** ≥ 4/5 on two different fables' quizzes.
- **If not yet:** shrink to one question word per session (all-WHO day); reread aloud together before asking.

## 5. Tools

- **Uses existing:** reader, parent quiz, research (offline Wikipedia), journal.
- **Blocked on new tool:** none — this is the first lesson chaining **reader → quiz → research → journal**, the integration pattern INTEGRATION.md formalizes.

## 6. Authoring notes

- Animation: none — the detective frame is parent-led.
- Quiz blueprint: `quiz.reading.k2.comp.01` (same per-fable banks; who/what/where items).
- Integration note: the research step is **germane, not decorative** — the fable's own animal, so the fact feeds back into tomorrow's retell.
