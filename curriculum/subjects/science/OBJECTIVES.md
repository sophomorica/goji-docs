**Misconceptions to watch (strand-level):** "it moves, so it's alive" (car, river, wind-blown leaf); "it doesn't move, so it's not alive" (tree, seed, sleeping cat); "it was once alive / part of something alive, so it's alive" (log, fallen leaf, wool); "things made to look alive are alive" (toy, robot, cartoon).
