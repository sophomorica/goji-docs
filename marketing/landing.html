Privacy

Their work stays in your house.

Everything a child makes on goji is stored on the device and nowhere else. We have no copy of it and no way to see it.

When goji is on Wi-Fi it sends us a short technical report so we can tell whether updates are installing. It has nothing about your child in it, and you can turn it off in Settings. goji keeps updating either way.

Updates

It gets better while it sits there.

New features arrive over Wi-Fi at no charge. goji installs them when nobody is using it, saves any unfinished work first, and puts the old version back by itself if anything goes wrong. No Wi-Fi? It keeps working exactly as it is.

Questions

Before you buy

Does it need the internet?

No. Every app works offline, including Wikipedia. Wi-Fi is only for updates, and you can skip it.

Can my child get to a website, a game store or YouTube?

No. There is no web browser and nothing to install. The part of goji your child uses cannot reach the internet at all.

What ages is it for?

Patrick to fill in: the age range you'll state.

Can more than one child use it?

Yes. Each child gets a profile with their own work and progress.

What can I, as a parent, change?

You choose a parent PIN during setup. Wi-Fi, updates, erasing the device and the technical report are behind that PIN.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. Updates are free.

Is there a phone app?

Not yet. A later update may add an optional parent app. If it does, nothing will leave the goji without your permission.

What if we don't like it?

Send it back within 30 days for a refund. You pay the return shipping.

What if something breaks?

Email us at support email. goji can save a log file to a USB stick for us to look at, and it can be erased and started fresh from Settings.

One price. Nothing to sign up for.

$400, shipping included in the United States. Confirm where you ship. Sales tax is added where it applies.
