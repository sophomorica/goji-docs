Quickstart card, first draft

Both sides of the card that goes in the box, drawn at 5 × 7 inches. The yellow marks are blanks to fill before it goes to a printer.

Front
Start here

Four steps. About five minutes.

  1. Connect the screenPlug the small end of the HDMI cable into the port next to the power port on goji. Plug the other end into your TV or monitor.
  2. Plug in a keyboard and mouseAny USB keyboard and mouse. Use any of the four USB ports.
  3. Plug in the power lastgoji turns on by itself. Switch your TV to the HDMI input you used. A red stamp appears while it starts.
  4. Answer three questionsChoose a parent PIN, type your child's name, and add Wi-Fi if you want updates. You can skip Wi-Fi.

Write your parent PIN down somewhere safe. We can't look it up for you. It protects Wi-Fi, updates and erasing the device.

Back
Good to know
Turning it off
Choose the power button on the home screen and wait for the screen to go dark before unplugging.
No picture
Check the TV is on the right HDMI input. Then move the cable to goji's other small HDMI port.
Updates
With Wi-Fi, goji updates itself when nobody is using it. Unfinished work is saved first.
Adding Wi-Fi or a child later
Open Settings (the gear on the home screen). You'll need your parent PIN.
Starting over
Settings → Help and support → Erase this Goji. It removes every profile and cannot be undone.
Need help?
Email support email. If we ask for a log file: Settings → Help and support → Save logs to USB.
Their work stays on this device.

goji has no web browser, no accounts and no ads. The only place it connects is our update server. Full policy: short link or QR code

Draft notes for Patrick (not part of the card)

  1. Check step 1 against the real unit in its case. I wrote "the port next to the power port" from the bare Pi 5 layout. If the case hides or relabels the ports, this wording has to change, and a small drawing of the back of the case would beat any sentence.
  2. Step 4 matches the setup wizard on main today: welcome, parent PIN, name, Wi-Fi, ready. If printing adds a printer step (it does on the printing branch), this becomes "four questions".
  3. Nobody has timed "about five minutes". The plan's exit test is under ten. Time it with the first person who isn't you, then set the number.
  4. "A red stamp appears while it starts" describes the boot splash. Confirm on the Pi 5 image.
  5. The privacy panel uses the same promise as the landing page, so the same caution applies: it depends on the lockdown work being merged and tested, and a lawyer should read it.
  6. Blanks: support email, and a short link or QR code to the policy.
  7. For print: this is a layout draft, not print-ready artwork. A printer will want a PDF with bleed and the fonts embedded.
